# Espionage Operations

## Overview
Spies gather intelligence, steal technology, and sabotage enemy infrastructure. Chameleons excel at espionage (+60% success).

---

## Spy Network

**Establishing Spies**:
- Cost: 50 BC per spy
- Placement: Assign to enemy empire
- Build-up Time: 5 turns to become effective

**Spy Effectiveness**:
- Base: 30% mission success
- +Espionage tech: +20-40%
- +Chameleons: +60%
- -Target security: -10-30%

**Example**: Chameleon with Advanced Espionage
= 30% + 60% + 40% = 130% (almost guaranteed success)

---

## Espionage Missions

### 1. Reconnaissance (Passive)
**Cost**: Free (spies in place)
**Success**: 80%
**Effect**: Gather intelligence
**Intel Gained**:
- Fleet locations and composition
- Technology level
- Production capacity
- Diplomatic relations

**Strategy**: Always active, no risk

### 2. Steal Technology
**Cost**: Varies (tech value)
**Success**: 30-60%
**Effect**: Randomly steal one technology
**Caught**: -20 relations, spy possibly executed

**Priority Targets**:
- Expensive techs (20,000+ RP)
- Weapons/shields you lack
- Special systems

**Chameleons**: Preferred method over research

### 3. Sabotage Factories
**Cost**: 100 BC
**Success**: 40%
**Effect**: Destroy 10-30 factories on target planet
**Caught**: -30 relations

**When to Use**:
- Before invasion (weaken production)
- Slow enemy shipbuilding
- Economic warfare

### 4. Incite Rebellion
**Cost**: 200 BC
**Success**: 20-40% (hard!)
**Effect**: Planet attempts to secede
**Requires**: Advanced Espionage tech
**Caught**: -50 relations, possible war

**Best Targets**:
- Recently conquered planets (low morale)
- Overcrowded planets
- Planets with high taxes

**Success**: Planet becomes independent or joins you

### 5. Frame Another Race
**Cost**: 150 BC
**Success**: 50%
**Effect**: Make it look like different race did espionage
**Requires**: Chameleon advanced tech
**Caught**: Massive scandal

**Strategy**:
- Turn two enemies against each other
- Cover your own operations
- Start wars without your involvement

**Chameleons**: Master this (False Flag operations)

### 6. Assassinate Leader (Optional Rule)
**Cost**: 300 BC
**Success**: 10% (very hard)
**Effect**: Enemy race suffers -20% all production for 10 turns
**Caught**: War declaration, -100 relations all races

**High Risk**: Only if desperate

## Counter-Espionage

**Defensive Spending**:
- Allocate BC to security
- Base: 10% spy detection
- +50 BC: +10% detection
- +150 BC: +25% detection
- +300 BC: +40% detection

**Catching Spies**:
- Detected: Relations penalty
- Executed: Additional penalty
- Demand apology: +10 relations if accepted

**Racial Security**:
- Ants: Immune to spies (hive-mind)
- Chameleons: Catch spies easily (+30% detection)
- Hamsters: Poor security (trusting)

---

## Technology Theft Strategy

**High-Value Targets**:
1. Robotic Controls IV (production boom)
2. Stellar Converter (superweapon)
3. Perfect Cloaking (invisibility)
4. Advanced Cloning (population explosion)

**Theft vs Research**:
- Chameleons: Steal everything
- Rats: Research faster than stealing
- Others: Steal what you're behind on

---

## Espionage Victory Strategy

**Chameleon Playbook**:
1. Establish spies Turn 20-30
2. Steal critical technologies
3. Frame races against each other
4. Watch galaxy burn
5. Swoop in during chaos

**Counter-Strategy vs Chameleons**:
- Maximum security spending
- Execute captured spies
- Form anti-Chameleon alliance
- Never trust them
