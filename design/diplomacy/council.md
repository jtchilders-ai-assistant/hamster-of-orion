# The High Council

## Overview
When 50%+ of galaxy colonized, the High Council forms to elect a Master of Orion through democratic vote. Win 2/3 majority = Diplomatic Victory.

---

## Council Formation

**Trigger**: >50% habitable planets colonized
**Frequency**: Every 25 turns after formation
**Attendance**: All living races (mandatory)
**Vote**: Elect Master of Orion OR Continue (no decision)

---

## Voting Mechanics

### Vote Weight
**Based on Population**:
- Each race votes = % of total galactic population
- Example: 30% population = 30% of vote

**Domination Link**: Population control = Council control

### Victory Requirement
**Need 2/3 Majority** (66.7%+)
- Own population + allied votes
- Abstentions don't count
- Rejection possible (continue game)

---

## Securing Votes

### Your Own Population
- Most reliable votes
- Increase through: Growth, conquest, expansion
- Rabbits naturally strong here (+100% growth)

### Alliance Votes
- Formal allies vote for you (usually)
- +50% relations minimum to consider
- Can still betray (Chameleons!)

### Bribery
- Offer BC/tech for votes
- Expensive but effective
- Poor races more receptive

### Intimidation
- Threaten war if don't vote for you
- Requires military superiority
- Guinea Pigs excel at this

### Racial Favoritism
- Hamsters: Others like them (+30 diplomacy)
- Guinea Pigs: Others fear them (intimidation)
- Chameleons: Others distrust them (-votes)

---

## Council Strategies by Victory Path

### Domination-Diplomatic Hybrid (Rabbits, Ants)
**Strategy**: Control 2/3 population yourself
- Don't need allies
- Pure demographic superiority
- Rabbits with Advanced Cloning can solo-vote

### Alliance Network (Hamsters, Rats)
**Strategy**: Build coalition
- Trade agreements with all
- Research pacts
- Mutual enemies
- Hamsters: +30% diplomacy makes this easy

### Intimidation (Guinea Pigs, Ferrets)
**Strategy**: Threaten everyone
- Massive military
- Recent conquests demonstrate power
- "Vote for me or else"
- Guinea Pigs natural intimidators

### Bribery (Mice, Economic Powers)
**Strategy**: Buy all votes
- Need massive economy
- Offer tech + BC
- Calculate costs vs benefits
- Mice's production supports this

---

## Vote Rejection

**Player Option**: Reject Council Decision
- If voted Master: Can refuse (continue game)
- If someone else voted Master: Can reject (declare war on them)

**Rejection = War**:
- All races choosing continuation unite
- vs. the would-be Master
- Fight for survival or conquest

**Strategy**:
- Accept if confident in position
- Reject if can still win militarily
- Guinea Pigs always reject (warriors don't negotiate)

---

## Council Meeting Events

**Pre-Vote Lobbying**:
- 5 turns before meeting
- Races approach you with deals
- Last-minute alliance offers
- Desperation bribes

**The Vote**:
- Dramatic presentation
- Each race declares vote
- Tension builds
- Victory or failure

**Post-Vote**:
- Winners celebrate
- Losers plot revenge
- Alliances shift
- Prepare for next vote

---

## Diplomatic Victory Requirements

**Cannot Win If**:
- Broken treaties recently (-50 turn penalty)
- Used bio-weapons (-75 relations all races)
- Destroyed planets (-50 relations all races)
- Treaty Breaker reputation

**Reputation Matters**: Honorable empires get more votes

---

## AI Council Behavior

**Will Vote For You If**:
- Allied (+80 relations)
- Intimidated (you're overwhelmingly strong)
- Bribed sufficiently
- Hate your opponent more

**Will Vote Against You If**:
- At war
- Broken treaties
- Racial enemy
- See you as threat

**Will Abstain If**:
- Neutral relations
- No strong preference
- Waiting to see outcome

---

## Multiple Council Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Landslide**
- You have 70%+ population
- No vote needed (automatic)
- Diplomatic Victory

**Scenario 2: Coalition**
- You + allies = 67%+
- Vote passes
- Diplomatic Victory

**Scenario 3: Close Vote**
- 60-66% support
- Swing votes critical
- Bribery decides

**Scenario 4: Rejection**
- <66% support
- Continue game
- Try again in 25 turns

**Scenario 5: Contested**
- Two candidates near 50%
- Split galaxy
- Civil war possible

---

## Council vs Other Victories

**Diplomatic**: Slowest but safest
- No massive wars needed
- Build alliances
- Turn 150-200 typical

**Domination**: Control population, auto-win Council
- Can skip diplomacy entirely
- Just need 2/3 population
- Rabbits/Ants path

**Discovery**: Bypass Council
- Conquer Orion before vote
- Instant victory
- Rats path

**Survival**: Make Council impossible
- Eliminate all races
- Last empire standing
- Guinea Pigs path
