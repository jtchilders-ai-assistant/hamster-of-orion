# AI Personalities & Behaviors

## Overview
Each race has distinct AI behavior patterns affecting diplomacy, warfare, expansion, and tech priorities.

---

## Personality Archetypes

### Hamsters - The Peacemakers
**Archetype**: Balanced Diplomat

**Expansion**: Moderate, peaceful colonization
**Military**: Defensive fleets, not aggressive
**Diplomacy**: Friendly to all, build alliances
**Technology**: Balanced research
**Trade**: Generous, fair deals

**Behavior Patterns**:
- Offers treaties early
- Rarely declares war first
- Accepts peace quickly
- Shares technology
- Mediates conflicts

**Player Strategy vs Hamsters**:
- Easy ally (trustworthy)
- Won't backstab
- Diplomatic victory easier with them
- Can be pushed around militarily

---

## Budgies - The Proud Warriors
**Archetype**: Combat Specialist

**Expansion**: Moderate, claims best systems
**Military**: Elite fighter fleets
**Diplomacy**: Respect strength, honor duels
**Technology**: Propulsion + Computers focus
**Trade**: Fair but dismissive

**Behavior Patterns**:
- Challenge rivals to "honorable combat"
- Respect military equals
- Disdain for cowards
- Keep warrior's code
- Never surrender easily

**Triggers War**:
- Border violations
- Insults to honor
- Weakness detected

**Player Strategy**:
- Show strength (get respect)
- Honor treaties (they do too)
- Don't appear weak
- Match their military

---

## Guinea Pigs - The Conquerors
**Archetype**: Aggressive Expansionist

**Expansion**: Rapid, through conquest
**Military**: Heavy dreadnoughts, massive armies
**Diplomacy**: Might makes right
**Technology**: Weapons + Construction only
**Trade**: Demands tribute, gives nothing

**Behavior Patterns**:
- Declare war frequently
- Demand surrender
- Keep treaties only if losing
- Intimidate weak neighbors
- Respect only strength

**Triggers War**:
- Proximity
- Weakness detected
- Resource competition
- Pretty much anything

**Player Strategy**:
- Build strong military (only deterrent)
- Don't show weakness
- Expect war eventually
- They keep warrior's honor (won't break peace treaty)

---

## Chameleons - The Infiltrators
**Archetype**: Manipulative Backstabber

**Expansion**: Opportunistic, sneaky
**Military**: Cloaked hit-and-run
**Diplomacy**: Lie constantly, frame others
**Technology**: Computers (espionage) + Cloaking
**Trade**: Steal rather than trade

**Behavior Patterns**:
- Friendly until profitable to betray
- Frame other races for crimes
- Steal technology constantly
- Break treaties without remorse
- Spy networks everywhere

**Triggers War**:
- Opportunity for gain
- Victim is weak
- Can blame someone else

**Player Strategy**:
- NEVER TRUST
- Maximum security spending
- Expect betrayal
- Form anti-Chameleon alliance
- Execute captured spies

---

## Ants - The Collective
**Archetype**: Efficient Expansionist

**Expansion**: Calculated, maximum efficiency
**Military**: Overwhelming numbers
**Diplomacy**: Logical calculations only
**Technology**: Construction + Biotech
**Trade**: Fair exchanges (exact value)

**Behavior Patterns**:
- Decisions based on efficiency calculations
- Perfectly reliable (no emotion)
- Expand methodically
- Trade fairly (to the BC)
- War when calculations favor it

**Triggers War**:
- Efficiency analysis says attack optimal
- Resources justify conflict
- Blocking optimal expansion path

**Player Strategy**:
- Logical negotiations work
- They honor treaties (no emotion to break)
- Expect numerical superiority
- Can't spy on them (hive-mind)

---

## Mice - The Technocrats
**Archetype**: Technology Specialist

**Expansion**: Selective, quality systems
**Military**: Small but advanced
**Diplomacy**: Pragmatic partnerships
**Technology**: Construction + Computers
**Trade**: Technology exchanges preferred

**Behavior Patterns**:
- Research focus
- Offer tech trades
- Reliable allies
- Calculate optimal alliances
- Update strategy based on data

**Triggers War**:
- Survival threatened
- Tech theft detected
- Calculations favor conflict

**Player Strategy**:
- Tech trading beneficial
- Reliable partners
- Respect technological achievement
- Don't steal from them (they hate that)

---

## Ferrets - The Hunters
**Archetype**: Opportunistic Predator

**Expansion**: Selective, valuable targets
**Military**: Alpha-strike cruisers
**Diplomacy**: Pragmatic but ruthless
**Technology**: Weapons + Computers
**Trade**: When beneficial only

**Behavior Patterns**:
- Strike weak targets
- Retreat from strong opponents
- Keep treaties while useful
- Betray when advantageous
- Respect fellow predators

**Triggers War**:
- Prey detected (weak neighbor)
- Easy conquest opportunity
- Resource competition

**Player Strategy**:
- Show strength (they respect it)
- Expect opportunism
- Don't appear weak
- They'll betray if profitable

---

## Rats - The Scientists
**Archetype**: Research Pacifist

**Expansion**: Minimal, quality over quantity
**Military**: Defensive only
**Diplomacy**: Cooperative, logical
**Technology**: ALL fields (research everything)
**Trade**: Enthusiastic tech sharing

**Behavior Patterns**:
- Research pacts offered early
- Avoid war (interrupts research)
- Share knowledge
- Fair trade partners
- Logical negotiations

**Triggers War**:
- Attacked first (defensive only)
- Research threatened
- No other option

**Player Strategy**:
- Easy ally (cooperative)
- Excellent tech trading partner
- Won't attack you
- Vulnerable to aggression (weak military)

---

## Rabbits - The Survivors
**Archetype**: Population Expansionist

**Expansion**: Everywhere, constant
**Military**: Swarm tactics
**Diplomacy**: Fearful, appeasing
**Technology**: Biotech + Propulsion
**Trade**: Accept any terms

**Behavior Patterns**:
- Accept any treaty for peace
- Terrified of strong neighbors
- Colonize everything
- Offer bribes to avoid war
- Reliable (too scared to break treaties)

**Triggers War**:
- Never (defensive only)
- Flee if possible
- Fight only if cornered

**Player Strategy**:
- Easy to intimidate
- Will give you anything for peace
- Demographic threat (population explosion)
- Can win Domination/Council through numbers

---

## Hermit Crabs - The Ancients
**Archetype**: Defensive Isolationist

**Expansion**: Slow, patient, selective
**Military**: Unkillable fortresses
**Diplomacy**: Distant, slow to trust
**Technology**: Force Fields + Construction
**Trade**: Rare but fair

**Behavior Patterns**:
- Minimal contact
- Defensive posture
- Keep treaties forever
- Patient strategy
- Wait for others to destroy each other

**Triggers War**:
- Direct attack only
- Territory violation
- Centuries of provocation

**Player Strategy**:
- Can ignore them safely
- Excellent defensive allies
- Slow to ally but eternal loyalty
- Near-impossible to conquer (defenses)

---

## AI Difficulty Modifiers

**Easy AI**:
- Makes suboptimal decisions
- Doesn't exploit weaknesses
- Accepts bad trades
- Forgives quickly

**Normal AI**:
- Plays to racial strengths
- Moderate exploitation
- Fair negotiations
- Typical behavior

**Hard AI**:
- Optimal decisions
- Exploits all weaknesses
- Ruthless negotiations
- Enhanced bonuses

**Impossible AI**:
- Cheats (+production/research)
- Perfect information
- Coordinated attacks
- Unfair advantages
