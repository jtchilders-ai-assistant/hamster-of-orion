# Hermit Crabs

## Physical Description
Soft organic bodies protected by crystalline shells of breathtaking beauty. Shells grow naturally, forming geometric patterns that refract light into rainbows. Each individual's shell is unique - their identity, their armor, their art. Patient, methodical movements. Eyes on stalks survey surroundings with alien calm.

## Biology & Habitat
- **Homeworld**: Crystalia (Harsh radiation-blasted world with silicon-based mineral life)
- **Environment Preference**: Any - shells provide complete environmental protection
- **Lifespan**: 50+ years (very long-lived)
- **Reproduction**: Asexual crystalline budding - extremely slow

## Culture & Society
### Core Values
Patience. Endurance. Self-sufficiency. "The shell protects. The shell provides. The shell endures." Beauty through function. Contemplation over action. All things in their proper time.

### Government Type
Contemplative oligarchy. The "Shell Council" consists of the eldest and largest-shelled individuals. Decisions made through meditation and consensus over weeks or months. Rush is alien concept.

### Philosophy on War
Violence is inefficient. Why fight when you can simply outlast? Shells protect from all harm. Time defeats all enemies eventually. Patience is the ultimate weapon. If conflict is necessary, simply endure until opponents exhaust themselves.

### View of Other Races
Fascinated by but confused by organic life's haste. View rapid-breeders (Rabbits, Ants) with wonder and concern. Appreciate Rats' contemplative nature. Puzzled by everyone's urgency. "The universe has existed for billions of years. Why the rush?"

## Gameplay Mechanics

### Racial Bonuses
- **Production**: +25% (efficient resource extraction)
- **Research**: +0% (balanced)
- **Food**: Not applicable (absorb minerals directly)
- **Growth**: -50% (extremely slow reproduction)
- **Ground Combat**: +25% (shells provide natural armor)
- **Ship Combat**: +0% (balanced)
- **Espionage**: -30% (too slow and visible)
- **Diplomacy**: +0% (patient but alien)

### Special Abilities
- **Universal Adaptation**: Can colonize ANY planet type without terraforming
- **Radiation Immunity**: Hostile environments don't affect production
- **No Food Requirement**: Don't need agriculture - frees up all farming slots for production/research
- **Armored Shell**: Natural +50% defense bonus on ground combat
- **Patient**: Immune to morale penalties and unrest
- **Mineral Consumption**: Can "eat" asteroids for resources

### Starting Technologies
- Nuclear Engines
- Controlled Environment (unnecessary but they have it anyway)
- Duralloy Armor (natural understanding)
- Automated Factory

### Unique Units/Buildings/Technologies
- **Unique Building**: Crystal Garden - Generates resources from planetary minerals
- **Unique Ship**: Fortress Class - Slow but heavily armored mobile station
- **Unique Technology**: Crystalline Matrix - Armor becomes stronger under fire

## AI Behavior

### Personality Archetype
Defensive / Expansion-Focused / Patient

### Diplomatic Tendencies
- **Natural Allies**: Rats (fellow contemplatives), Hamsters (patient diplomats)
- **Natural Enemies**: None (conflict is temporary)
- **Trade Behavior**: Fair but slow to negotiate
- **Treaty Reliability**: Absolute - promises are eternal

### Strategic Priorities
1. Colonize hostile worlds others cannot
2. Build impregnable defenses
3. Expand methodically across entire galaxy
4. Research defensive technologies
5. Simply outlast all opponents

### War Behavior
Purely defensive. Never attacks. Builds massive fortifications. Sits in shells and waits. Enemies eventually give up or exhaust themselves. Time is always on Hermit Crabs' side. Will wait decades for peace if necessary.

## Design Notes
Hermit Crabs are the "patient survivor" race - can colonize anywhere, extremely defensive, wins through outlasting. Perfect for players who love tall empires and defensive gameplay.

Design intent: Unique resource-free gameplay. No food requirements means 100% of planetary resources go to production and research. Can colonize death worlds others can't touch.

Their universal adaptation is game-changing - every planet is habitable. While others fight over Garden and Terran worlds, Hermit Crabs colonize radioactive hellscapes, toxic wastelands, and barren rocks.

Balance consideration: Slow growth (-50%) means small population empire. Must win through efficiency and positioning, not numbers. Vulnerable to early rushes before defenses are established.

Key weakness: Very slow expansion despite colonizing anywhere. Takes decades to field large fleets. Cannot pursue fast victory strategies. Must commit to long game.

## Repurposed Ancient Technology
Hermit Crabs have contemplated Ancient facilities for over a century. Their conclusion (reached after 47 years of meditation): the structures are eternal monuments to patience and endurance.

The exercise wheels demonstrate perpetual motion - energy that endures forever. The Habitrail tubes show interconnection transcending space. The food dispensers prove that needs can be met without struggle.

The Ancient Ones did not disappear - they simply slowed down until they achieved perfect stillness and became one with the universe itself. The Hermit Crabs believe they are following the same path.

"Orion does not need to be conquered or discovered. It simply needs to be reached by those patient enough to journey to the center. We have time. We have always had time."

## Unique Gameplay Note
Hermit Crabs don't experience food as a resource. Instead, their "food" slider adjusts mineral absorption efficiency. This makes their resource management unique - they can min-max production and research without agricultural concerns.
