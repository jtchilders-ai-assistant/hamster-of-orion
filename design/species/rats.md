# Rats

## Physical Description
Larger than mice, with longer snouts and tails. Large, intelligent eyes that gleam with curiosity. Constantly grooming whiskers used as sensory instruments. Often seen manipulating multiple tools simultaneously. Obsessive note-takers and data collectors.

## Biology & Habitat
- **Homeworld**: Scientifica (Planetwide university - more research labs than living quarters)
- **Environment Preference**: Climate-controlled academic environments
- **Lifespan**: 4 years (natural), but consciousness archived in data banks
- **Reproduction**: Discouraged (interferes with research time), cloning preferred

## Culture & Society
### Core Values
Knowledge above all. Understanding is the highest good. Ignorance is the only true evil. "The universe is a puzzle to be solved, not a territory to be conquered."

### Government Type
Academic meritocracy. The "Peer Review Council" consists of the most published and cited researchers. Debates are scholarly arguments backed by data. Leadership changes with major breakthroughs.

### Philosophy on War
War is a fascinating problem in applied physics and game theory. Study it academically. If forced to fight, use superior technology derived from better understanding. Prefer research to conflict.

### View of Other Races
Everyone is either a research subject or potential collaborator. Frustrated by Guinea Pigs (anti-intellectual). Fascinated by Ants (hive-mind is unprecedented dataset). View Mice as "applied researchers" (said somewhat condescendingly). Competitive with each other.

## Gameplay Mechanics

### Racial Bonuses
- **Production**: -10% (focus on labs over factories)
- **Research**: +50% (unmatched scientific capability)
- **Food**: +0% (balanced)
- **Growth**: -10% (reproduction interferes with research)
- **Ground Combat**: -20% (poor fighters, better thinkers)
- **Ship Combat**: +10% (superior technology compensates)
- **Espionage**: +15% (good at information gathering)
- **Diplomacy**: +10% (rational negotiators)

### Special Abilities
- **Genius Researchers**: Technology costs 50% less to research
- **Academic Network**: Always gets multiple tech choices per field
- **Eureka Moments**: Occasional free breakthrough technologies
- **Quick Study**: Can reverse-engineer captured tech immediately
- **Scientific Method**: Immune to false intelligence from Chameleons

### Starting Technologies
- Nuclear Engines
- Deep Space Scanner (superior sensors)
- Research Laboratory Mark I
- Standard Colony Base

### Unique Units/Buildings/Technologies
- **Unique Building**: Grand University - Massive research bonus, attracts scientist population
- **Unique Ship**: Research Vessel - Unarmed scout that generates research points
- **Unique Technology**: Unified Field Theory - Unlocks all remaining tech tree branches

## AI Behavior

### Personality Archetype
Researcher / Peaceful Expansionist / Opportunistic

### Diplomatic Tendencies
- **Natural Allies**: Mice (fellow researchers), Hamsters (rational diplomats)
- **Natural Enemies**: Guinea Pigs (anti-intellectual barbarians)
- **Trade Behavior**: Enthusiastic about research agreements
- **Treaty Reliability**: Very High - logical and contractual

### Strategic Priorities
1. Research everything possible
2. Expand to high-research planets
3. Establish academic treaties with all races
4. Build defensive fleet (minimal investment)
5. Win through technological transcendence

### War Behavior
Avoids war aggressively. Builds defensive fleets only. If attacked, deploys technologically superior weapons with devastating efficiency. Seeks peace quickly to resume research. Views war as "unfortunate interruption of important work."

## Design Notes
Rats are the "pure research" race - fastest tech advancement in the game but weak at everything requiring brawn. They can rush to end-game technology before others leave mid-game.

Design intent: Technology race gameplay. Research everything, stay 2-3 tech levels ahead, win through sheer innovation. Perfect for players who love tech trees and long-term planning.

Their research bonus (+50%) is massive and compounds over time. By late game, Rats have technologies no one else can match. However, their military weakness means they must survive early aggression.

Balance consideration: Godlike late-game power but extremely vulnerable early. Guinea Pigs or Rabbits can crush them before research advantage kicks in. Must negotiate, hide, or beg for protection until tech superiority is established.

Key weakness: Poor at military conquest. Must pursue Diplomatic victory (negotiate council votes) or use technological superiority to eventually overwhelm enemies for Domination victory.

## Repurposed Ancient Technology
Rats have extensively studied Ancient facilities and published 47,000+ peer-reviewed papers on the subject. Current consensus: Ancient Ones achieved technological singularity and transcended physical reality through pure knowledge accumulation.

All Ancient structures are teaching tools - cosmic textbooks left for successor civilizations. The Habitrail tubes encode hyperspatial mathematics in their geometry. The exercise wheels demonstrate perpetual motion principles (if properly analyzed). The food dispensers are molecular assembly proof-of-concepts.

Rats have learned more from studying Ancient technology than any other race. They believe they're closest to understanding - and potentially reversing - whatever process transformed the Ancient Ones.

"We will not merely find Orion. We will understand Orion. Understanding is greater than possession."
