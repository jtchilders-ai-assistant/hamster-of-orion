# Chameleons

## Physical Description
Color-shifting scales that adapt to any environment. Independently rotating eyes allowing 360-degree vision. Long, prehensile tail. Slow, deliberate movements that mask their true nature. No one has ever seen their "true" coloration - if such a thing exists.

## Biology & Habitat
- **Homeworld**: Chromatia (Dense jungle world with perpetual fog)
- **Environment Preference**: High humidity, low visibility, complex terrain
- **Lifespan**: 15 years (natural), extended through clandestine cloning
- **Reproduction**: Solitary egg layers, slow population growth

## Culture & Society
### Core Values
Information is power. Trust no one. Survival through deception. "The seen blade never cuts deepest." Paranoia is wisdom. Everyone is either an asset or a threat.

### Government Type
Shadow Directorate - no one knows who truly leads. Official spokespersons are puppets. Real power brokers operate from the shadows. Even Chameleons don't know who gives the orders.

### Philosophy on War
Direct warfare is failure of intelligence. True victory is won before the first shot. Why conquer when you can infiltrate? Why destroy when you can subvert? Open war is admission of incompetence.

### View of Other Races
Everyone is a potential mark. Trust is a weakness to exploit. They particularly enjoy manipulating Guinea Pigs (so predictable) and Hamsters (so naive). Frustrated by Ants (hive-mind, can't infiltrate). Wary of other Chameleons.

## Gameplay Mechanics

### Racial Bonuses
- **Production**: -10% (focus on espionage over industry)
- **Research**: +10% (stolen technology and intelligence)
- **Food**: +0% (balanced)
- **Growth**: -10% (slow reproduction, secretive)
- **Ground Combat**: +0% (balanced)
- **Ship Combat**: -10% (avoid direct confrontation)
- **Espionage**: +60% (masters of infiltration)
- **Diplomacy**: +20% (master manipulators)

### Special Abilities
- **Master Spies**: Espionage missions cost 50% less and succeed more often
- **Infiltrators**: Can see enemy technology, production, and fleet movements
- **False Flags**: Can frame other races for espionage acts
- **Sleeper Agents**: Sabotage delayed by several turns, impossible to trace
- **Technology Theft**: Stealing tech is easier than researching it

### Starting Technologies
- Hyper-X Rockets (fast, stealthy scouts)
- ECM Jammer Mark I
- Stealth Suit (ground infiltration)
- Battle Scanner (intelligence gathering)

### Unique Units/Buildings/Technologies
- **Unique Building**: Intelligence Network - Provides constant intel on all races
- **Unique Ship**: Shadow Class - Invisible to most sensors
- **Unique Technology**: Perfect Mimicry - Can disguise ships as other races' vessels

## AI Behavior

### Personality Archetype
Sneaky / Espionage-Focused / Unpredictable

### Diplomatic Tendencies
- **Natural Allies**: None (trusts no one, but pretends to be everyone's friend)
- **Natural Enemies**: None (avoids open conflict)
- **Trade Behavior**: Uses trade to gather intelligence
- **Treaty Reliability**: Very Low - breaks treaties frequently, frames others

### Strategic Priorities
1. Establish spy networks in all empires
2. Steal technology rather than research
3. Manipulate races into fighting each other
4. Expand through subversion and infiltration
5. Avoid direct military confrontation

### War Behavior
Avoids open war. Uses sabotage, assassination, and false flag operations. If forced into war, relies on stolen technology and guerrilla tactics. Prefers to make peace quickly, then resume covert operations.

## Design Notes
Chameleons are the "espionage specialist" race - weak in direct confrontation but devastating through spies and subterfuge. They play a completely different game from other races.

Design intent: Complex, paranoid gameplay. Information warfare, technology theft, diplomatic manipulation. For players who enjoy scheming and indirect strategies.

Their espionage bonus (+60%) is massive, making them the undisputed masters of intelligence. They can see everything their opponents are doing and steal technology faster than they can research it.

Balance consideration: Weak military means they crumble in direct assault. Must rely on manipulation and keeping enemies fighting each other. If the galaxy unites against them, they lose.

Key weakness: Cannot fight multiple coordinated enemies. Require time to infiltrate and subvert. Early game rushes can crush them before their spy networks are established.

## Repurposed Ancient Technology
Chameleons believe the Ancient Ones were master spies who left these facilities as training grounds for future intelligence operatives. They claim to have "infiltrated" Orion centuries ago and know what's inside, but refuse to share details. This is likely a bluff - probably. Maybe. No one knows for certain, which is exactly how they want it.

The Habitrail tubes? Obviously a covert transportation network. The exercise wheels? Clearly interrogation devices (though this seems paranoid even for Chameleons). The water bottles? Dead drop locations for secret messages.
