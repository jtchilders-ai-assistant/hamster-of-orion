# Guinea Pigs

## Physical Description
Stocky, muscular build despite small size. Dense fur in browns, blacks, and whites. Powerful legs built for charging. Intimidating despite being herbivores. Their eyes burn with warrior intensity.

## Biology & Habitat
- **Homeworld**: Cavissia (High-gravity world with rugged mountains)
- **Environment Preference**: Heavy gravity, harsh climates, challenging terrain
- **Lifespan**: 6 years (natural), extended through warrior's cloning rituals
- **Reproduction**: Large litters, but population growth controlled by warrior culture

## Culture & Society
### Core Values
Strength, honor, courage, glory in battle. "A warrior's worth is measured in scars and victories." Death in battle is the highest honor. Weakness is shameful.

### Government Type
Military oligarchy. The strongest warriors form the War Council. Leadership earned through combat trials and battlefield achievement. Challenges to authority resolved through ritual combat.

### Philosophy on War
War is the natural state. Peace is merely preparation for the next conflict. Combat should be direct, face-to-face, and brutal. Orbital bombardment without invasion is cowardly. True warriors fight on the ground.

### View of Other Races
Respect strength wherever found. Contemptuous of weak races (Hamsters, Rabbits). Grudging respect for Ferrets and Budgies. View Chameleons as dishonorable cowards. Consider Ants interesting but disturbing.

## Gameplay Mechanics

### Racial Bonuses
- **Production**: +10% (strong workers)
- **Research**: -20% (anti-intellectual culture)
- **Research Field Bonuses**: Weapons +20%, Construction +20% (matches MOO1 Bulrathi — better infantry gear and ship armor, thematically fitting)
- **Food**: +0% (efficient herbivores)
- **Growth**: +10% (large litters)
- **Ground Combat**: +50% (devastating infantry)
- **Ship Combat**: +10% (brave but not tactical)
- **Espionage**: -20% (too direct and honorable)
- **Diplomacy**: -20% (aggressive and intimidating)

### Special Abilities
- **Warrior Culture**: Ground troops deal +50% damage and have higher morale
- **Fearless**: Immune to morale penalties from losing battles
- **Heavy Worlders**: Can colonize high-gravity worlds at no penalty
- **Relentless**: Conquered planets integrate faster (less resistance)

### Starting Technologies
- Hyper-V Rockets (powerful but simple)
- Reinforced Hull
- Hand Lasers (ground combat)
- Duralloy Armor

### Unique Units/Buildings/Technologies
- **Unique Building**: War Academy - Doubles ground troop effectiveness
- **Unique Ship**: Dreadnought Class - Heavily armored assault ship
- **Unique Technology**: Battle Frenzy - Ground troops become even more devastating in prolonged combat

## AI Behavior

### Personality Archetype
Aggressive / Expansionist / Militaristic

### Diplomatic Tendencies
- **Natural Allies**: Budgies (mutual respect), Ferrets (fellow warriors)
- **Natural Enemies**: Chameleons (dishonorable), Hamsters (weak diplomats)
- **Trade Behavior**: Uninterested - prefers taking what they want
- **Treaty Reliability**: Medium - will break treaties if they sense weakness

### Strategic Priorities
1. Build massive ground armies
2. Expand aggressively through conquest
3. Subjugate weaker neighbors
4. Maintain large production base
5. Research military technologies only

### War Behavior
Extremely aggressive. Declares war frequently. Always invades rather than bombing. Throws wave after wave of troops at planets. Poor at naval combat but compensates with overwhelming force. Accepts only unconditional surrender.

## Design Notes
Guinea Pigs are the "warrior brute" race - devastating ground forces but poor at everything intellectual. They excel at invasion but struggle with research and diplomacy.

Design intent: Straightforward aggressive gameplay. Build armies, conquer planets, repeat. Perfect for players who want simple, direct combat without complex strategy.

Their research penalty (-20%) is severe, keeping them technologically behind. They compensate through overwhelming numbers and superior ground combat.

Balance consideration: They're terrifying early-game (when ground combat matters most) but fall behind in late-game as technology becomes more important. Their poor diplomacy means everyone hates them.

Key weakness: Vulnerable to advanced weapons they can't research fast enough. Rely on capturing enemy technology through conquest.

## Repurposed Ancient Technology
Guinea Pigs believe the Ancient Ones were the ultimate warrior race who created these facilities as training grounds. They view the exercise wheels as "endurance training devices" and force all warriors to run in increasingly large wheels as punishment and conditioning. The water bottles are seen as "hydration discipline stations" - warriors must earn their water through combat prowess.
