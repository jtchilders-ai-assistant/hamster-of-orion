# Hamsters

## Physical Description
Golden-brown fur, large cheek pouches, bright black eyes. Average 6 inches tall in their natural form, though they often wear environmental suits for space travel. Remarkably expressive faces despite their small size.

## Biology & Habitat
- **Homeworld**: Hamsteria Prime (Terran-type world with extensive grasslands)
- **Environment Preference**: Temperate, moderate humidity, prefer underground burrows
- **Lifespan**: 3 years (natural), extended indefinitely through cloning and consciousness transfer
- **Reproduction**: Fast breeders, but practice population control

## Culture & Society
### Core Values
Balance, adaptability, survival through cooperation, practical diplomacy. "A full pouch and a safe burrow" is their cultural ideal.

### Government Type
Representative democracy with a rotating council of elders. The "Eternal Leader" consciousness provides continuity while elected officials handle day-to-day governance.

### Philosophy on War
Pragmatic pacifists. "War is a failure of imagination." They fight only when necessary, prefer defensive strategies, and always seek peaceful resolution. However, when threatened, they're surprisingly tenacious.

### View of Other Races
Optimistic but not naive. Believe all races can coexist if they find common ground. View themselves as natural mediators and diplomats.

## Gameplay Mechanics

### Racial Bonuses
- **Production**: +0% (balanced)
- **Research**: +0% (balanced)
- **Food**: +10% (efficient foragers)
- **Growth**: +0% (balanced)
- **Ground Combat**: -10% (small and not naturally aggressive)
- **Ship Combat**: +0% (balanced)
- **Espionage**: -20% (too honest for subterfuge)
- **Diplomacy**: +30% (natural mediators)

### Special Abilities
- **Universal Diplomat**: All other races start at Neutral rather than Unfriendly
- **Trade Hub**: +20% credits from trade agreements
- **Council Favorite**: +1 vote in High Council elections
- **Adaptive**: Can colonize any planet type at reduced penalty

### Starting Technologies
- Nuclear Engines (better than standard retro rockets)
- Controlled Environment (can colonize wider range)
- Standard Missiles
- Titanium Armor

### Unique Units/Buildings/Technologies
- **Unique Building**: Galactic Embassy - Increases diplomatic relations and trade income
- **Unique Ship**: Cargo Runner - Unarmed transport that generates credits from trade routes
- **Unique Technology**: Cultural Exchange Program - Further boosts diplomatic relations

## AI Behavior

### Personality Archetype
Diplomat / Balanced Expander

### Diplomatic Tendencies
- **Natural Allies**: All races (tries to befriend everyone)
- **Natural Enemies**: None (never declares war first)
- **Trade Behavior**: Fair and balanced, seeks mutual benefit
- **Treaty Reliability**: Extremely high - almost never breaks treaties

### Strategic Priorities
1. Establish diplomatic relations with all races
2. Secure trade agreements
3. Moderate expansion (not aggressive)
4. Research balanced across all fields
5. Build defensive fleet (never attacks first)

### War Behavior
Purely defensive. Builds strong fortifications and fleets but only attacks if directly threatened. Prefers to let enemies come to them. Will accept reasonable peace terms quickly.

## Flavor & Personality

### Leader Names
*Friendly, approachable names*
- Males: Whiskers, Nibbles, Pip, Chester, Fuzzy, Buttons
- Females: Daisy, Peanut, Cinnamon, Honey, Marble
- Dynasty Numbers: I, II, III, etc.

### Ship Names
*Optimistic and cheerful*
- HSS Unity, Harmony, Prosperity, Abundance
- HSS Golden Field, Safe Harbor, Warm Burrow
- HSS Cooperation, Friendship, Alliance

### Planet Names
*Named after comfortable concepts*
- New Warren, Safe Haven, Plenty, Comfort, Serenity
- Also use: Alpha, Beta, Gamma for colonies

### Quotes & Personality
**When greeting player:**
> "Greetings, friend! We extend our paw in friendship. The galaxy is vast - surely there is room for all of us?"

**When declaring war (only if attacked):**
> "We sought only peace, but you have left us no choice. Even the smallest creature will fight when cornered."

**When forming alliance:**
> "Together we are stronger than apart! This alliance honors both our peoples."

**When betraying player (extremely rare):**
> "You gave us no choice. We will remember this day with sadness."

**When defeated:**
> "We accept defeat with dignity. Perhaps in defeat, we can still find a path to peace. Our descendants will remember your... efficiency."

## Design Notes
Hamsters are the "human equivalent" race - balanced, diplomatic, flexible. They're the easiest race for new players because they don't have major penalties and their diplomatic bonuses make the early game more forgiving.

The design intent is "Switzerland in Space" - everyone's friend, neutral haven, benefits from peace and trade rather than conquest.

Their weakness is that they're mediocre at everything except diplomacy. Expert players might find them boring, but they're perfect for teaching game mechanics.

## Repurposed Ancient Technology
Hamsters believe the Ancient Ones were benevolent caretakers who left these facilities as gifts for their descendants. They view habitrail tubes as "The Great Network" - proof that the universe is interconnected. Their cultural mythology centers on the idea that they were meant to bring all races together, just as the tubes once connected all habitats.
