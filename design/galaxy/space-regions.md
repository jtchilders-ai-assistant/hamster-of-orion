# Space Regions

## Overview
The galaxy is divided into four distinct regions from the lore, each with unique characteristics.

---

## 1. The Safe Zones (Home Regions)

**Location**: Map edges, starting areas
**Systems**: 30% of galaxy
**Planet Quality**: Best available
**Strategic Role**: Foundation of empires

**Characteristics**:
- Highest concentration of Terran/Ocean worlds
- No Barren/Dead planets
- Rich worlds possible
- Peaceful early game (until borders meet)

**Homeworlds**: All placed here
- Terran or Ocean, Large/Huge
- Pre-developed (factories, population)
- Cannot be destroyed by Stellar Converter

**Strategy**:
- Build core economy here
- Maximum factories and research
- Heavily defend borders

**Flavor**: "These systems are ours. They have always been ours. They will always be ours."

---

## 2. The Wild Pellet Fields (Contested Space)

**Location**: Middle ring, most of galaxy
**Systems**: 50% of galaxy
**Planet Quality**: Mixed, competitive
**Strategic Role**: Expansion and conflict

**Characteristics**:
- All planet types possible
- Most Artifacts worlds here
- Gaia worlds (if any) here
- Heavily contested

**Conflict Zone**: Where empires collide
- Border wars
- Diplomatic negotiations
- Resource competition

**Strategy**:
- Grab what you can
- Defend key systems
- Negotiate or fight
- Every system matters

**Flavor**: "The Ancient Ones scattered their pellet dispensers across the galaxy. Now we fight over the crumbs."

---

## 3. The Dark Sectors (Nebula Regions)

**Location**: Random pockets throughout galaxy
**Systems**: 15% of galaxy
**Planet Quality**: Poor, hostile
**Strategic Role**: Hidden treasures and danger

**Characteristics**:
- Nebula clouds (shields don't work)
- High concentration Toxic/Radiated
- Sensor penalties
- Hidden Artifacts worlds

**Combat Hazard**: Shields disabled
- Armor-based defenses essential
- Ambush opportunities
- High-risk exploration

**Hidden Value**:
- Artifacts worlds hidden here
- Rich mineral deposits
- Enemies won't look here

**Strategy**:
- Late-game expansion
- Hermit Crabs excel (don't need shields)
- Hide retreating fleets here
- Unexpected bases

**Flavor**: "The darkness hides many things. Some of them are treasures. Some of them are tombs."

---

## 4. The Omega Sector (Orion's Domain)

**Location**: Galactic center, surrounding Orion
**Systems**: 5% of galaxy, forbidden
**Planet Quality**: Unknown (sensors blocked)
**Strategic Role**: End-game objective

**Characteristics**:
- **Energy Barrier Masking**: Sensors cannot penetrate the Orion region until the Guardian is defeated or a fleet enters the system (though entering triggers combat). Scans from outside return static.
- **Buffer Clearing**: There is a forced 3-parsec buffer around Orion where no other star systems may spawn, isolating it completely.
- Guardian patrols intercept any fleet entering the system.
- Unknown dangers wait for the unwary.

**Orion System**:
- Planet Orion at exact center
- Guardian orbits
- Cosmic Wheel on surface
- Major strategic milestone (not a victory condition)

**Approach Strategy**:
- Don't attempt before Turn 100
- Need advanced propulsion (Ion Drive minimum)
- Need powerful fleet (5+ Dreadnoughts)
- One attempt only (losing fleet = disaster)

**Flavor**: "At the center of everything, the Wheel turns. The Guardian waits. Will you be worthy?"

## Regional Strategies by Race

### Safe Zone Dominance (Turtling)
**Best Races**: Hermit Crabs, Hamsters, Mice
**Strategy**: 
- Claim Safe Zone completely
- Build impregnable core
- Defend borders, don't expand further
- Tech/production advantage

### Pellet Field Expansion (Aggressive)
**Best Races**: Guinea Pigs, Rabbits, Ants
**Strategy**:
- Rush into Pellet Fields
- Claim maximum territory
- Fight all neighbors
- Quantity over quality

### Dark Sector Infiltration (Sneaky)
**Best Races**: Chameleons, Rats
**Strategy**:
- Avoid main conflicts
- Colonize Dark Sectors
- Hidden research bases
- Surprise attacks from darkness

### Orion Rush (All-In)
**Best Races**: Rats, Ferrets, Budgies
**Strategy**:
- Tech rush to Guardian-killing weapons
- Build fleet in secret
- One massive push for Orion
- Gain massive tech advantage to win Diplomatic or Domination

---

## Regional Control (Strategic Advantages)

Controlling regions provides strategic bonuses:
- Safe Zones: Secure core territory
- Pellet Fields: Resource abundance
- Dark Sectors: Hidden bases, surprise attacks
- Omega: Orion technologies (after defeating Guardian)

## Regional Events

**Safe Zone Events**:
- Trade convoys
- Peaceful discoveries
- Pirates (rare)

**Pellet Field Events**:
- Border incidents
- Space monsters
- Ancient ruins discovered

**Dark Sector Events**:
- Fleet disappearances
- Sensor ghosts
- Ancient warnings

**Omega Sector Events**:
- Guardian sightings
- Energy anomalies
- System failures (Orion's power)
