# Exploration Mechanics

## Overview
The galaxy starts covered in fog of war. Send scouts to reveal star systems, find planets, and discover opportunities.

---

## Fog of War

**Unexplored Systems**: Black, no information
**Explored Systems**: Visible, planet data known
**Enemy Territory**: Visible only if scouted recently

**Scanner Range**:
- Default: 1 system (adjacent only)
- Deep Space Scanner: 3 systems
- Advanced Scanner: 5 systems
- Planetary Scanner: Detect incoming fleets

---

## Scout Ships

**Role**: Exploration vessels
**Speed**: Fast (prioritize propulsion tech)
**Combat**: Weak (flee from threats)
**Cost**: Cheap (50-100 BC)

**Scout Loadout**:
- Best engine available
- Scanner systems
- NO weapons (waste of space)
- ECM or Cloaking (survival)

**Strategy**:
- Build 5-10 early game
- Fan out in all directions
- Prioritize nearest systems
- Report back before exploring deep

---

## System Discovery

**First Contact**:
1. Scout enters unexplored system
2. System revealed on map
3. Star type identified
4. Planets scanned (basic info)
5. Scout reports findings

**Information Gained**:
- Number of planets
- Planet types (Terran, Arid, etc.)
- Planet sizes (Tiny to Huge)
- Special features (Asteroid belt, nebula)

**Detailed Scan** (requires tech):
- Minerals (Rich/Normal/Poor)
- Artifacts presence
- Exact population capacity

---

## Exploration Priority

**Turn 1-10**: Immediate vicinity
- Find nearby colonizable planets
- Identify threats
- Establish safe expansion zone

**Turn 10-30**: Expansion targets
- Find best planets within 10 parsecs
- Identify chokepoints
- Locate enemy positions

**Turn 30+**: Deep space
- Map entire galaxy
- Find Artifacts worlds
- Locate Orion
- Identify invasion routes

---

## Colonization Window

**Race for Planets**: First colony ship wins
- Scout finds planet
- Rush colony ship before enemy
- Plant flag, claim system

**Contested Systems**:
- Both empires can reach
- Diplomatic crisis
- Often triggers first war

---

## Ancient Ruins Discovery

**Artifacts Worlds**: 
- 25% chance: Technology discovered
- 50% chance: Research bonus only
- 25% chance: Nothing (ruins too damaged)

**Possible Discoveries**:
- Advanced weapons
- Special systems
- Defensive tech
- Immediate breakthrough

**Strategy**: Always explore ruins immediately

---

## Space Monsters

**Rare Encounters** (5% of systems):
- Space Amoeba (regenerates, slow)
- Crystal Entity (reflects beams)
- Space Dragon (powerful, guards treasure)

**Defeat Rewards**:
- Technology
- Resources
- System becomes safe

**Strategy**: Avoid until fleet strong enough

---

## Exploration Hazards

**Nebulae**: Shields don't work (combat risk)
**Black Holes**: Navigation hazard (can't enter)
**Solar Flares**: Damage scouts (10% chance)
**Pirates**: Hostile ships (rare, early game)

---

## Strategic Scouting

**Budgies**: +1 movement = best scouts
**Chameleons**: Cloaked scouts = spy enemy territory
**Rabbits**: Many cheap scouts = full map coverage
**Hermit Crabs**: Slow but thorough

---

## Discovery Milestones

**Milestone 1**: Home region mapped (Turn 5-10)
**Milestone 2**: Enemy homeworld located (Turn 15-25)
**Milestone 3**: Artifacts worlds found (Turn 30-50)
**Milestone 4**: Orion discovered (Turn 50-80)
**Milestone 5**: Full galaxy mapped (Turn 80-120)
