# Planetology

## Overview
Planetology governs the transformation of worlds, colonization of hostile environments, and biological warfare. It is the key to expanding the population of the empire and maximizing the productivity of every star system.

**Key Races**: Rabbits (+100% Growth Rate), Ants (+25% Growth), Hermit Crabs (Natural colonization of Hostile worlds)

## Tactical Strategy

**Population Explosion**: Terraforming is the single most important tech for long-term power. More people = more production, research, and votes.
**Expansion Pressure**: Controlled Environment tech allows you to claim systems that other races cannot yet touch, effectively "locking off" sectors of the galaxy.
**The Biological Choice**: Biological weapons are devastating but carry massive diplomatic penalties. Use with extreme caution.
