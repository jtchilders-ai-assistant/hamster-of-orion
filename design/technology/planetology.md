# Planetology Technology - Complete Tech Tree

## Overview

The Planetology technology field governs the transformation of worlds, colonization of hostile environments, population growth, ecological restoration, and biological warfare. It is the key to expanding the population capacity of the empire and maximizing productivity across every star system.

**Key Races:**
- Rabbits: +100% Population Growth Rate
- Ants: +50% Production (rapid industrialization of colonies)
- Hermit Crabs: Natural hostile environment immunity (colonize any planet)

**Technology Tiers:** 1-18 (Starting Free to 40,000+ RP)

---

In each game, players are offered **2-3 random technologies** at each tier. Not all technologies appear in every game, creating strategic variety.

## Technology Categories

### Terraforming
Increases maximum population capacity on all planets empire-wide.

### Soil Enrichment
Permanently improves planet base size and growth rate.

### Environmental Colonization
Unlocks ability to colonize increasingly hostile planetary environments.

### Ecological Restoration
Reduces pollution cleanup costs from industrial waste.

### Biological Weapons
Ship-mounted weapons that kill planetary population (devastating but diplomatically catastrophic).

### Bio-Defense
Antidotes that reduce casualties from enemy biological attacks.

### Cloning
Accelerates population growth through artificial reproduction.

### Atmospheric Terraforming
Converts hostile planets to habitable environments.

## Detailed Category Statistics

### Terraforming Progression

Terraforming permanently increases the maximum population capacity of planets. The cost is paid per million population added.

| Technology | Tech Level | RP Cost | Max Pop Bonus | Cost per Million |
|------------|------------|---------|---------------|------------------|
| Improved Terraforming +10 | 2 | 60 | +10 | 5 BC |
| Improved Terraforming +20 | 7 | 1,020 | +20 | 5 BC |
| Improved Terraforming +30 | 12 | 3,130 | +30 | 4 BC |
| Improved Terraforming +40 | 18 | 6,400 | +40 | 4 BC |
| Improved Terraforming +50 | 24 | 10,810 | +50 | 3 BC |
| Improved Terraforming +60 | 28 | 16,380 | +60 | 3 BC |
| Improved Terraforming +80 | 32 | 23,100 | +80 | 2 BC |
| Improved Terraforming +100 | 36 | 30,970 | +100 | 2 BC |
| Complete Terraforming | 38 | 40,000 | +120 | 2 BC |

**Terraforming Formula:**
```
Terraforming_Cost = Population_Bonus × Cost_Per_Million
```

**Example:** Terraforming +30 on a planet costs 30 × 4 = 120 BC total.

---

### Soil Enrichment Progression

Soil enrichment permanently improves a planet's base characteristics. Effects do not stack - Advanced replaces basic.

| Technology | Tech Level | RP Cost | Base Size Bonus | Growth Bonus | One-Time Cost |
|------------|------------|---------|-----------------|--------------|---------------|
| Soil Enrichment | 14 | 4,090 | +25% | +50% | 150 BC |
| Advanced Soil Enrichment | 26 | 14,400 | +50% | +100% (Gaia) | 300 BC |

**Soil Enrichment Effects:**
- **Base Size Bonus:** Increases the planet's base population capacity before terraforming
- **Growth Bonus:** Multiplies natural population growth rate
- **Gaia Conversion:** Advanced Soil Enrichment converts planets to Gaia environment type

**Example:** A size 80 Normal planet with Advanced Soil Enrichment:
- Base size: 80 × 1.50 = 120
- Growth rate: 2.0× (Gaia equivalent)
- With Complete Terraforming (+120): Total capacity = 240

---

### Environmental Colonization Progression

Each technology unlocks colonization of a specific hostile environment type. Hermit Crabs ignore these requirements and can colonize any planet from game start.

| Technology | Tech Level | RP Cost | Environment Type | Hostility Level |
|------------|------------|---------|------------------|-----------------|
| (Base) | — | — | Standard environments | None |
| Controlled Barren Environment | 3 | 140 | Barren | 1 (least hostile) |
| Controlled Tundra Environment | 6 | 570 | Tundra | 2 |
| Controlled Dead Environment | 8 | 1,290 | Dead | 3 |
| Controlled Inferno Environment | 10 | 2,300 | Inferno | 4 |
| Controlled Toxic Environment | 13 | 3,600 | Toxic | 5 |
| Controlled Radiated Environment | 17 | 5,180 | Radiated | 6 (most hostile) |

**Environment Hostility Effects:**
- Hostile environments have 0.5× population growth rate
- Standard environments have 1.0× population growth rate
- Gaia environments have 2.0× population growth rate

**Atmospheric Terraforming:**
- Tech Level 21, 7,740 RP, 200 BC one-time cost
- Converts hostile planets to standard minimal environment
- Normalizes population growth from 0.5× to 1.0×
- Does NOT stack with Soil Enrichment (apply enrichment after atmosphere conversion)

### Ecological Restoration Progression

Eco restoration reduces the BC cost to clean up industrial pollution. Pollution is generated by factories (1 waste per factory per turn at baseline).

| Technology | Tech Level | RP Cost | Waste Eliminated per BC |
|------------|------------|---------|-------------------------|
| Ecological Restoration | 1 | Free | 2 waste / 1 BC |
| Improved Eco Restoration | 4 | 400 | 3 waste / 1 BC |
| Enhanced Eco Restoration | 11 | 2,700 | 5 waste / 1 BC |
| Advanced Eco Restoration | 22 | 9,210 | 10 waste / 1 BC |
| Complete Eco Restoration | 29 | 18,490 | 20 waste / 1 BC |

**Pollution Cleanup Formula:**
```
Cleanup_Cost = Total_Waste / Waste_Per_BC
```

**Example:** A planet with 100 factories generates 100 waste.
- With Ecological Restoration: 100 / 2 = 50 BC to clean
- With Complete Eco Restoration: 100 / 20 = 5 BC to clean

**Note:** Construction tech "Reduced Industrial Waste" reduces waste GENERATED. Eco Restoration reduces cleanup COST. They complement each other.

### Biological Weapons

Bio weapons are ship-mounted special weapons that kill planetary population. Each attack reduces the target planet's population. They carry severe diplomatic penalties.

| Weapon | Tech Level | RP Cost | Kill Rate | Space | Cost |
|--------|------------|---------|-----------|-------|------|
| Death Spores | 9 | 1,600 | 1M per attack | 150 | 100 BC |
| Doom Virus | 25 | 11,660 | 2M per attack | 200 | 200 BC |
| Bio Terminator | 33 | 25,600 | 3M per attack | 250 | 300 BC |

**Bio Weapon Mechanics:**
- Ship must have bio weapon equipped in design
- Attack reduces population each combat round the ship survives
- Multiple bio weapons stack (2 Death Spores = 2M per attack)
- Does NOT affect factories or planetary defenses
- Kills defenders during ground invasion phase

**Diplomatic Penalty:**
- Using bio weapons causes -100 relation with ALL races
- Some races will declare war immediately
- Council members may vote to condemn

**Bio Weapon Formula:**
```
Population_Killed = Weapon_Kill_Rate × Number_Of_Weapons × Combat_Rounds_Survived
```

**Example:** A ship with 2 Death Spores survives 3 combat rounds:
- 1M × 2 × 3 = 6M population killed

---

### Bio-Defense (Antidotes)

Antidotes reduce casualties from enemy biological weapon attacks.

| Technology | Tech Level | RP Cost | Casualty Reduction |
|------------|------------|---------|-------------------|
| Bio Toxin Antidote | 15 | 4,620 | -1M per attack |
| Universal Antidote | 30 | 20,730 | -2M per attack |

**Bio Defense Formula:**
```
Actual_Casualties = Max(0, Bio_Weapon_Damage - Antidote_Reduction)
```

**Example:** Enemy attacks with Doom Virus (2M), defender has Bio Toxin Antidote (1M reduction):
- 2M - 1M = 1M casualties

**Example:** Enemy attacks with Death Spores (1M), defender has Universal Antidote (2M reduction):
- Max(0, 1M - 2M) = 0 casualties (immune)

---

### Cloning Technology

Cloning allows direct BC investment to grow population faster than natural growth.

| Technology | Tech Level | RP Cost | Population per BC |
|------------|------------|---------|-------------------|
| Cloning | 19 | 7,050 | 1M per 10 BC |
| Advanced Cloning | 34 | 28,220 | 1M per 5 BC |

**Cloning Formula:**
```
Population_Grown = BC_Invested × Cloning_Rate
```

**Example:** With Advanced Cloning, investing 50 BC:
- 50 BC × (1M / 5 BC) = 10M population grown

**Cloning Synergy with Rabbits:**
- Rabbits have +100% natural growth rate
- Cloning adds ON TOP of natural growth
- Combined effect: Explosive population growth

## Strategic Importance

### Early Game (Tiers 1-4)
**Critical Priority:** Controlled Environment Technologies
- **Controlled Barren (T2):** Unlock ~20% more colonizable worlds
- **Controlled Tundra (T3):** Unlock additional ~15% more worlds
- **Terraforming +10 (T2):** First population capacity boost
- **Improved Eco (T3):** Reduce cleanup costs as factories grow

### Mid Game (Tiers 5-7)
**Focus:** All environments + growth acceleration
- **Controlled Dead/Inferno/Toxic:** Complete hostile colonization
- **Soil Enrichment:** +25% capacity on all worlds
- **Cloning:** Fill new colonies instantly
- **Atmospheric Terraforming:** Convert hostiles to normal growth

### Late Game (Tiers 8-12)
**Focus:** Maximize capacity and consider bio weapons
- **Advanced Soil Enrichment:** Gaia conversion
- **Complete Terraforming:** +120 capacity per planet
- **Bio Weapons:** Devastate enemy populations (use carefully)
- **Complete Eco Restoration:** Near-zero cleanup costs

## Formulas Summary

### Terraforming Cost
```
Total_Cost = Population_Bonus × Cost_Per_Million
```

### Soil Enrichment Effect
```
New_Base_Size = Original_Base_Size × (1 + Size_Bonus)
New_Growth_Rate = Base_Growth × (1 + Growth_Bonus) × Environment_Modifier
```

### Pollution Cleanup
```
Cleanup_Cost = ceil(Factory_Waste / Waste_Per_BC)
```

### Bio Weapon Damage
```
Population_Lost = (Kill_Rate × Weapon_Count × Rounds) - Antidote_Reduction
Population_Lost = max(0, Population_Lost)
```

### Cloning Growth
```
Population_Grown = BC_Invested / BC_Per_Million
```

## Edge Cases

### Hermit Crabs and Hostile Worlds
- Can colonize without tech, but still suffer 0.5× growth
- Atmospheric Terraforming still benefits them (normalizes growth)
- Soil Enrichment converts to Gaia for 2.0× growth

### Maximum Population
```
Max_Population = (Base_Size × Soil_Bonus) + Terraforming_Bonus
```
- Cannot exceed hard cap of 300 per planet
- Base sizes range from 10 (tiny) to 120 (huge)

### Stacking Bio Weapons
- Multiple bio weapons on same ship stack additively
- Multiple ships attack in sequence (each gets full rounds)
- Antidote applies to total damage, not per-weapon

### Bio Weapons vs Empty Planets
- Cannot target planets with 0 population
- Bio weapons have no effect on factories/defenses

### Terraforming Already Terraformed
- New terraforming tech replaces old (takes you to new limit)
- Cost is for TOTAL new capacity, not difference
- Example: Planet at +10, upgrade to +30 costs full 30 × 4 BC

## Examples

### Example 1: Terraforming a Planet

**Situation:** Medium Terran planet (base size 80), with Improved Terraforming +30

**Calculation:**
```
Terraforming Cost = 30 × 4 BC/million = 120 BC
New Max Population = 80 + 30 = 110
```

### Example 2: Full Planet Development

**Situation:** Large Ocean planet (base size 100), with Advanced Soil Enrichment + Complete Terraforming

**Calculation:**
```
Step 1: Apply Advanced Soil Enrichment
  - Cost: 300 BC (one-time)
  - New Base Size: 100 × 1.50 = 150
  - Environment: Converted to Gaia (2.0× growth)

Step 2: Apply Complete Terraforming (+120)
  - Cost: 120 × 2 BC/million = 240 BC
  - Final Max Population: 150 + 120 = 270

Total Investment: 540 BC
Final Capacity: 270 (near maximum of 300)
```

### Example 3: Bio Weapon Attack

**Situation:** Fleet with 3 Death Spores attacks planet with Bio Toxin Antidote, survives 4 rounds

**Calculation:**
```
Gross Casualties = 1M × 3 weapons × 4 rounds = 12M
Antidote Reduction = 1M per attack (applied per round)
Net Reduction = 1M × 4 rounds = 4M
Final Casualties = 12M - 4M = 8M population killed
```

### Example 4: Rabbit Cloning Colony

**Situation:** Rabbits with Advanced Cloning invest 100 BC into population growth on a new colony

**Calculation:**
```
Cloned Population = 100 BC / 5 BC per million = 20M
Plus natural growth (doubled for Rabbits)
If base growth = 0.1M/turn × 2.0 = 0.2M/turn natural
Total first-turn growth potential: 20.2M
```
