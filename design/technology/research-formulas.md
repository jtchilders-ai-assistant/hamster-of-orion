# Research Point Calculation

## Overview

Research is how empires unlock new technologies in Hamster of Orion. Research Points (RP) are generated by scientists and research buildings, then pooled empire-wide and allocated to technologies. This document specifies exact formulas for RP generation, tech costs, miniaturization, and all related mechanics.

**Key MOO1 Faithful Mechanics:**
- RP is pooled empire-wide (not per-planet)
- Scientists are population allocated to research via slider
- Research Labs increase RP output per scientist
- Tech cost scales with tier (exponential)
- Miniaturization makes older tech smaller/cheaper
- 2-3 random tech choices per tier

---

## Core Formulas

### 1. Research Points Per Planet

Each planet generates RP based on scientists and research infrastructure:

```
Planet_RP = Scientists × Base_RP_Per_Scientist × Research_Lab_Multiplier × Racial_Modifier × Difficulty_Modifier
```

Where:
- `Scientists` = Population assigned to research (via slider)
- `Base_RP_Per_Scientist` = 1.0 RP/turn
- `Research_Lab_Multiplier` = Bonus from research buildings
- `Racial_Modifier` = Race-specific research bonus
- `Difficulty_Modifier` = Game difficulty adjustment

---

### 2. Research Lab Multipliers

Research buildings increase the RP output per scientist:

| Technology Level | Building | Multiplier | Cumulative |
|------------------|----------|------------|------------|
| 0 (None) | No Lab | 1.0× | 1.0× |
| 1 | Research Lab | +0.5× | 1.5× |
| 10 | Supercomputer | +1.0× | 2.5× |
| 25 | Autolab | +1.5× | 4.0× |
| 40 | Galactic Cybernet | +2.0× | 6.0× |

**Note:** These bonuses are cumulative - each new building adds to the total multiplier.

**Example:** A planet with Research Lab and Supercomputer has a 2.5× multiplier.

### 4. Empire-Wide Research Pool

All planetary RP is combined into a single empire pool:

```
Empire_Total_RP = Σ(Planet_RP) for all planets + Bonus_RP

Bonus_RP = Artifact_Bonuses + Event_Bonuses + Orion_Bonus
```

#### Special RP Bonuses

| Source | Bonus |
|--------|-------|
| Artifacts World | +25% RP from that planet |
| Orion (if colonized) | +400% RP (4× research speed) |
| Random Discovery Event | +100-1000 RP one-time |
| Research Treaty | +10% of partner's RP (mutual) |

---

### 5. RP Allocation to Technologies

The Empire RP pool is divided among active research projects using percentages:

```
RP_To_Field = Empire_Total_RP × (Field_Slider_Percentage / 100)
```

Each of the 6 technology fields has a slider:
- Weapons
- Propulsion
- Construction
- Computers
- Force Fields
- Planetology

**Constraint:** All field percentages must sum to 100%.

The actual cost may be modified by various factors:

```
Actual_Tech_Cost = Base_Tech_Cost × (1 / Racial_Research_Modifier) × Galaxy_Size_Modifier × Difficulty_Modifier
```

### 8. Research Progress Tracking

```
Research_Progress[field] += RP_To_Field (each turn)

If Research_Progress[field] >= Actual_Tech_Cost:
    Tech_Completed = True
    Research_Progress[field] -= Actual_Tech_Cost
    # Overflow RP carries to next tech
```

## Miniaturization

### 9. Miniaturization Formula (MOO1 Faithful)

As you advance in a technology field, older technologies become smaller and cheaper:

```
Size_Reduction = min((Current_Tier - Tech_Tier) × Miniaturization_Rate, Maximum_Reduction)
Miniaturized_Size = Base_Size × (1 - Size_Reduction)
Miniaturized_Cost = Base_Cost × (1 - Size_Reduction)
```

Where:
- `Current_Tier` = Your highest researched tier in that field
- `Tech_Tier` = The tier of the component being miniaturized
- `Miniaturization_Rate` = 0.05 (5% per tier)
- `Maximum_Reduction` = 0.50 (50% cap, matching MOO1)

**Minimum Size:** Components cannot shrink below 50% of their original size (0.5× minimum).

**Note:** MOO1 capped miniaturization at 50% reduction. This design choice is preserved for balance.

#### Miniaturization Example

You have Tier 8 Weapons research. You want to use a Tier 3 weapon:
```
Size_Reduction = (8 - 3) × 0.05 = 0.25 (25% smaller)
Miniaturized_Size = 10 × (1 - 0.25) = 7.5 space
Miniaturized_Cost = 50 × (1 - 0.25) = 37.5 BC
```

---

### 10. Miniaturization by Field

Miniaturization applies differently per field:

| Field | What Miniaturizes | Effect |
|-------|-------------------|--------|
| Weapons | Beam, Missile, Bomb | Size and cost reduced |
| Propulsion | Engines, Fuel Tanks | Size reduced (cost N/A) |
| Construction | Armor | Size reduced slightly |
| Computers | Battle Computers, ECM | Size and cost reduced |
| Force Fields | Shields, Specials | Size and cost reduced |
| Planetology | N/A | Does not miniaturize |

## Tech Selection System

### 11. Random Tech Choices

When you complete a tech tier, you're offered 2-3 random choices for the next tier:

```
Available_Choices = random_select(All_Tier_Techs, count=2 or 3)
```

The number of choices depends on race and random factors:
- **Base:** 2 choices
- **Rats:** Always 3 choices (Academic Network ability)
- **Random Event:** May grant extra choice

### 12. Tech Trees Are Per-Game

Not all technologies appear in every game:
- Each field has ~15-20 possible techs
- Only ~60% are available per game
- Creates strategic variability
- Espionage and trading can fill gaps

---

### 14. Building Construction Priority

When auto-building research infrastructure:

```
If current_building == None:
    build Research Lab
Else if current_building == Research Lab AND tech_level >= 10:
    upgrade to Supercomputer
Else if current_building == Supercomputer AND tech_level >= 25:
    upgrade to Autolab
Else if current_building == Autolab AND tech_level >= 40:
    upgrade to Galactic Cybernet
```

## Special Research Mechanics

### 15. Tech Stealing (Espionage)

When stealing technology:

```
Steal_Success_Chance = Base_Chance × Spy_Skill × Racial_Modifier × Tech_Difficulty

Tech_Difficulty = 1.0 - (Target_Tech_Tier × 0.03)
```

Stolen techs:
- Instantly complete
- Do not count toward miniaturization
- May be techs not in your tree

### 16. Tech Trading

Techs can be traded through diplomacy:

```
Trade_Value = Base_Tech_Cost × (1 + (Current_Tier - Tech_Tier) × 0.1)
```

Trade rules:
- Both parties must agree
- Some races refuse military tech trades
- Traded techs count toward miniaturization

### 17. Reverse Engineering

Captured ships can be reverse-engineered:

```
Reverse_Engineer_Time = Tech_Cost × 0.25 (turns to understand)
```

Special abilities:
- **Rats:** Instant reverse engineering
- **Mice:** 50% faster reverse engineering

## Algorithm: Calculate Empire Research

```pseudocode
function calculate_empire_research(empire):
    total_rp = 0
    
    for planet in empire.planets:
        # Get scientists (from population slider)
        scientists = planet.population * (planet.research_slider / 100)
        
        # Get research building multiplier
        lab_multiplier = 1.0
        for building in planet.buildings:
            if building.type == "research":
                lab_multiplier += building.rp_bonus
        
        # Calculate planet RP
        racial_modifier = get_racial_research_modifier(empire.race)
        
        planet_rp = scientists * 1.0 * lab_multiplier * racial_modifier
        
        # Apply special bonuses
        if planet.has_artifacts:
            planet_rp *= 1.25
        if planet.is_orion:
            planet_rp *= 4.0
        
        total_rp += planet_rp
    
    # Apply research treaties
    for treaty in empire.treaties:
        if treaty.type == "research_pact":
            partner_rp = calculate_empire_research(treaty.partner)
            total_rp += partner_rp * 0.10
    
    return total_rp
```

## Algorithm: Process Research Turn

```pseudocode
function process_research_turn(empire):
    total_rp = calculate_empire_research(empire)
    
    for field in technology_fields:
        # Get allocation from field slider
        allocation_percent = empire.research_allocation[field]
        field_rp = total_rp * (allocation_percent / 100)
        
        # Get current research target
        current_tech = empire.current_research[field]
        if current_tech == None:
            continue  # No research in this field
        
        # Get tech cost
        base_cost = get_tech_tier_cost(current_tech.tier)
        galaxy_mod = get_galaxy_size_modifier(game.galaxy_size)
        actual_cost = base_cost * galaxy_mod
        
        # For Rats: tech costs 50% less (modifier 1.5 applied to RP, not cost)
        # Actually applied via racial modifier on RP generation
        
        # Add RP to progress
        empire.research_progress[field] += field_rp
        
        # Check for completion
        if empire.research_progress[field] >= actual_cost:
            # Complete tech
            empire.completed_techs.append(current_tech)
            empire.current_tier[field] = current_tech.tier
            
            # Overflow to next tech
            overflow = empire.research_progress[field] - actual_cost
            empire.research_progress[field] = overflow
            
            # Offer new tech choices
            new_tier = current_tech.tier + 1
            choices = get_random_tech_choices(field, new_tier, empire)
            empire.pending_tech_choices[field] = choices
            
            # Trigger UI notification
            notify_tech_complete(empire, current_tech)
```

## Algorithm: Miniaturization

```pseudocode
function get_miniaturized_stats(component, empire):
    field = component.technology_field
    component_tier = component.tech_tier
    current_tier = empire.current_tier[field]
    
    # Calculate reduction
    tier_difference = current_tier - component_tier
    reduction = tier_difference * 0.05  # 5% per tier
    
    # Apply maximum (MOO1 caps at 50%)
    reduction = min(reduction, 0.50)  # Cannot reduce more than 50%
    
    miniaturized_size = component.base_size * (1 - reduction)
    miniaturized_cost = component.base_cost * (1 - reduction)
    
    return {
        size: max(miniaturized_size, component.base_size * 0.50),
        cost: max(miniaturized_cost, component.base_cost * 0.50)
    }
```

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Basic RP Calculation

**Setup:**
- Race: Hamsters (1.0× modifier)
- Planet Population: 100
- Research Slider: 30%
- Buildings: Research Lab, Supercomputer
- No special bonuses

**Calculation:**
1. Scientists: 100 × 0.30 = 30
2. Lab multiplier: 1.0 + 0.5 + 1.0 = 2.5×
3. Racial modifier: 1.0
4. Planet RP: 30 × 1.0 × 2.5 × 1.0 = **75 RP/turn**

---

### Example 2: Rats Research Advantage

**Setup:**
- Race: Rats (1.5× modifier)
- Empire: 5 planets, each with 50 population, 40% research
- All planets have Research Lab + Supercomputer

**Calculation per planet:**
1. Scientists: 50 × 0.40 = 20
2. Lab multiplier: 2.5×
3. Racial modifier: 1.5
4. Planet RP: 20 × 1.0 × 2.5 × 1.5 = 75 RP

**Empire total:** 75 × 5 = **375 RP/turn**

Compared to Hamsters (same setup):
- Hamster RP: 20 × 1.0 × 2.5 × 1.0 = 50 RP per planet
- Hamster total: 50 × 5 = 250 RP/turn
- **Rats research 50% faster**

---

### Example 3: Miniaturization

**Setup:**
- Current Weapons tier: 12
- Want to use Tier 5 weapon (Fusion Beam)
- Fusion Beam base size: 20 space
- Fusion Beam base cost: 15 BC

**Calculation:**
1. Tier difference: 12 - 5 = 7
2. Size reduction: 7 × 0.05 = 0.35 (35%)
3. Miniaturized size: 20 × (1 - 0.35) = 13 space
4. Miniaturized cost: 15 × (1 - 0.35) = 9.75 BC → **10 BC**

---

### Example 4: Tech Cost with Galaxy Size

**Setup:**
- Researching Tier 10 tech (base cost: 6,000 RP)
- Large galaxy (1.25× modifier)
- Race: Guinea Pigs (0.80× research efficiency)

**Calculation:**
1. Adjusted cost: 6,000 × 1.25 = 7,500 RP

**Time to research:**
- Guinea Pigs generate 100 RP/turn (example)
- But research efficiency 0.80× means effectively 80 RP/turn
- Turns to complete: 7,500 / 80 = **~94 turns**

Compare to Rats (1.5× efficiency, same base RP):
- Effective RP: 100 × 1.5 = 150 RP/turn
- Turns to complete: 7,500 / 150 = **50 turns**

## Edge Cases

### No Scientists
If no population is allocated to research:
- Empire RP = 0
- No research progress
- Tech choices remain pending

### Research Overflow
When tech completes with excess RP:
- Overflow applies to next tech
- If no next tech selected, overflow is stored
- Stored RP applies when player selects new research

### All Techs Researched
When all techs in a field are complete:
- Slider allocation goes to "Reserve Research"
- Reserve can be converted to BC (50% efficiency)
- Or held for future discoveries

### Mid-Turn Tech Choice
If player hasn't selected tech for a field:
- RP accumulates in "pending" pool
- Applied when player makes selection

### Artifacts Destroyed
If planet with Artifacts is bombed:
- Artifacts are destroyed
- Bonus lost permanently

## Difficulty Effects on Research

### Player Research (All Difficulties)
- Tech costs are **unchanged**
- RP generation is **unchanged**
- Player experience is consistent

### AI Research by Difficulty

| Difficulty | AI Research Rate | Effect |
|------------|------------------|--------|
| Simple | 0.50× | AI researches much slower |
| Easy | 0.75× | AI researches slower |
| Average | 1.00× | Baseline |
| Hard | 1.50× | AI researches faster |
| Impossible | 2.00× | AI researches much faster |

This is implemented as a cost reduction, not RP bonus.
