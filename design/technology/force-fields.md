# Force Fields Technology

## Overview
Force fields provide primary defense through ship-mounted Deflector Shields, Planetary Shields for colony protection, and special Tactical Fields that disrupt enemy movement and targeting.

**Key Races**: Hermit Crabs (+25% Defense), Hamsters (Balanced Defense)

## Tactical Strategy

**Shield Hardening**: Small, high-firepower weapons (like Lasers or Gatling Lasers) are completely negated by high-class Deflector Shields.
**Planetary Defense**: A world with a Class XX Shield and high-tier Missile Bases is effectively immune to anything but high-damage Bombs or Torpedoes.
**The Repulsor Trick**: Using Repulsor Beams to keep slow, short-range enemy ships or bombardment-only ships at bay can win battles against superior numbers.
