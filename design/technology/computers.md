# Computers Technology - Complete Tech Tree

## Overview

The Computers technology field governs targeting accuracy (Battle Computers), missile defense (ECM), industrial automation (Robotic Controls), scanning and intelligence, and special targeting systems. It is one of six technology fields and serves as a critical force multiplier for both combat and economy.

**Key Races:**
- Budgies: +3 Initiative, superior pilots
- Chameleons: +60% Espionage effectiveness
- Rats: +50% Research (discover computers tech faster)

**Technology Tiers:** 1-18 (50 RP to 50,000 RP)

---

## Tech Tree Structure

In each game, players are offered **2-3 random technologies** at each tier. Not all technologies appear in every game, creating strategic variety.

### Tier Distribution

| Tier Range | Tech Level | Game Phase | Typical RP Cost |
|------------|------------|------------|-----------------|
| 1-3 | 1-8 | Early | 50-150 RP |
| 4-6 | 9-17 | Early-Mid | 250-800 RP |
| 7-9 | 18-27 | Mid | 1,500-4,000 RP |
| 10-12 | 28-37 | Mid-Late | 6,000-10,000 RP |
| 13-15 | 38-45 | Late | 14,000-24,000 RP |
| 16-18 | 46-50 | End | 30,000-50,000 RP |

---

## Technology Categories

### Battle Computers
Increase weapon accuracy (Attack Rating).

### ECM Jammers
Reduce enemy missile hit chance (Missile Defense Rating).

### Robotic Controls
Increase factories per population ratio.

### Scanners
Detection range and intelligence gathering.

### Special Systems
Unique targeting and combat abilities.

## Racial Computer Bonuses

### Budgies: Superior Pilots
- +3 Initiative in all combat
- Natural piloting ability enhances computers
- Battle Computers are even more effective

### Chameleons: Master Spies
- +60% Espionage effectiveness
- Scanners reveal more information
- Can steal computer technologies easily

### Rats: Academic Brilliance
- +50% Research speed
- Discover computer tech faster than anyone
- Will have best computers earliest

## Strategic Importance

### Early Game (Tiers 1-4)
**Critical Priority:** Robotic Controls III
- **RC II → III:** +50% factory capacity is massive
- **Battle Computer II-III:** Ensures hits connect
- **Deep Space Scanner:** Early warning for attacks

### Mid Game (Tiers 5-9)
**Focus:** Combat effectiveness
- **BC V-VII:** Serious accuracy advantage
- **ECM V-VII:** Counter missile fleets
- **RC IV-V:** Economic multiplication
- **Hyperspace Comm:** Fleet flexibility

### Late Game (Tiers 10-14)
**Focus:** Overwhelming advantage
- **BC VIII-XI:** Near-perfect accuracy
- **ECM VIII-X:** Missiles become useless against you
- **Oracle Interface:** All weapons penetrate armor
- **RC VI-VII:** Industrial dominance

## Computer vs Computer Dynamics

### Attack Rating vs ECM

| Attacker BC | Defender ECM | Net Modifier |
|-------------|--------------|--------------|
| +5 | +3 | +2 (attacker advantage) |
| +5 | +5 | 0 (neutral) |
| +3 | +5 | -2 (defender advantage) |

### Technology Arms Race

The Computers field creates an arms race:
1. High BC makes your shots hit
2. High ECM makes their missiles miss
3. Scanners reveal enemy tech levels
4. Plan your research accordingly
