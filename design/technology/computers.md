# Computers Technology

## Overview
Computers technology governs targeting accuracy (Attack Rating), missile defense (ECM), industrial capacity (Robotic Controls), and scanning/intelligence. It is a critical force multiplier for both ship combat and economic growth.

**Key Races**: Budgies (Superior Pilots), Chameleons (+60% Espionage), Ferrets (+30% Ship Combat)

## Tactical Strategy

**Offensive Build**: Focus on Battle Computers to ensure heavy hits connect.
**Economic Rush**: Focus on Robotic Controls to out-produce opponents.
**Defensive Build**: ECM Jammers paired with high maneuverability make your ships nearly unhittable by missiles.

**Critical Intelligence**: Scanners are vital for knowing where to deploy fleets before an invasion arrives.
