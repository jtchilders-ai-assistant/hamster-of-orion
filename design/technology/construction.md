# Construction Technology

## Overview
Construction technology governs ship armor, industrial productivity (factory costs), and environmental sustainability (pollution control). It provides the physical and economic durability required for a galactic empire.

**Key Races**: Ants (+50% Production), Mice (+25% Production, +15% Research), Guinea Pigs (+50% Ground Combat)

## Tactical Strategy

**Production Momentum**: Lowering factory costs early (Industrial Tech) allows for exponential economic growth.
**Waste Management**: Essential for late-game industrial hubs where pollution would otherwise swallow production.
**Survival**: Upgrading armor is the most direct way to keep expensive capital ships alive under heavy fire.
