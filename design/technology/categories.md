# Technology Categories - Detailed Explanation

## 1. Weapons Technology

### Purpose
Offensive capabilities for ships and planetary bombardment.

### What It Unlocks
- **Beam Weapons**: Instant-hit, no ammo needed, damage varies by range
  - Lasers (early) → Gatling Lasers (mid) → Ion Cannons → Phasors → Plasma (late) → Mauler Device (ultimate)
  - Used for ship-to-ship combat

- **Missile Weapons**: Projectiles that can be shot down, fixed damage
  - Nuclear → Hyper-V → Fusion → Antimatter → Zeon Missiles
  - Torpedoes: Anti-Matter and Plasma (cannot be intercepted)

- **Bombs**: For planetary bombardment and invasion support
  - Nuclear → Fusion → Antimatter → Neutronium Bombs
  - Reduces planetary defenses and population

- **Special Weapons**: Unique capabilities
  - Neutron Blaster, Death Spores, Doom Virus, Bio Terminator
  - Often have special effects beyond damage (e.g., killing population)

### Strategy Considerations
- **Beam vs Missile**: Beams can't be intercepted, missiles do consistent damage
- **Weapon Size**: Bigger weapons need bigger ships
- **Mix and Match**: Different weapon types counter different defenses

### Race Specializations
- **Ferrets**: All weapons deal +25% damage
- **Guinea Pigs**: Prefer bigger, slower weapons
- **Budgies**: Prefer fast-firing, light weapons

## 2. Propulsion Technology

### Purpose
Movement, speed, and fuel efficiency across the galaxy.

### What It Unlocks
- **Engine Types**: Determine interstellar speed
  - Retro → Nuclear → Fusion → Ion → Anti-Matter → Inter-Phased → Hyper Drive

- **Fuel Cells**: Determine maximum range from colony
  - Range 4 → 10, Thorium Cells (Infinite)
  - Critical for reaching distant systems

- **Tactical Devices**: Combat bonuses and movement
  - Inertial Stabilizer, Energy Pulsar, Warp Dissipator, Sub-Space Teleporter, Displacement Device
  - Enhances survival and combat positioning

### Strategy Considerations
- **Early Exploration**: Better propulsion = claim more systems first
- **Strategic Mobility**: Respond to threats faster
- **Combat Advantage**: Faster ships fire first, dodge better
- **Orion Accessibility**: Need high-tier propulsion to reach galaxy center

### Race Specializations
- **Budgies**: +1 movement range on all ships
- **Rabbits**: Fast colony ships for rapid expansion
- **Chameleons**: Stealth ships need good engines to escape

## 3. Construction Technology

### Purpose
Building ships, armor, factories, and infrastructure.

### What It Unlocks
- **Armor Types**: Ship HP and Ground Combat bonuses
  - Titanium → Duralloy → Zortrium → Andrium → Tritanium → Adamantium → Neutronium
  - Better armor = more hit points + invasion protection

- **Industrial Efficiency**: Reducing manufacturing costs
  - Industrial Tech 9 → Tech 2 (reduces factory cost from 10 BC to 2 BC)
  - Massive boost to empire-wide production

- **Waste Reduction**: Environmental stability
  - Reduced Industrial Waste 80% → 20%, Waste Elimination
  - Essential for high-production worlds to avoid pollution

### Strategy Considerations
- **Production Power**: More factories = more ships/turn
- **Ship Durability**: Better armor keeps ships alive longer
- **Economic Foundation**: Construction tech powers everything else
- **Scaling**: Compounds over time - early advantage grows

### Race Specializations
- **Ants**: +50% factory output
- **Mice**: Automated factories, robotic labor
- **Hermit Crabs**: Natural armor bonuses

## 4. Computers Technology

### Purpose
Targeting accuracy (Attack Rating), electronic countermeasures (ECM), and industrial scaling (Robotic Controls).

### What It Unlocks
- **Battle Computers**: +1 to +11 Attack Rating bonuses
- **ECM Jammers**: +1 to +10 Missile Defense Rating
- **Robotic Controls**: Increases factory:pop ratio from 2:1 up to 7:1
- **Scanners**: Detect fleets and view ship/planet stats at a distance
- **Special**: Communication relays and tech nullifiers

### Strategy Considerations
- **Accuracy vs Evasion**: Offense vs Defense tradeoff
- **Information Advantage**: See enemy coming, prepare defenses
- **Espionage War**: Steal tech, sabotage production
- **Force Multiplier**: Makes your weapons/ships more effective

### Race Specializations
- **Ferrets**: Superior targeting, weapons fire first
- **Chameleons**: +60% espionage, steal everything
- **Budgies**: Combat computers boost dogfighting

## 5. Force Fields Technology

### Purpose
Defensive shields, planetary protection, and tactical tactical battlefields.

### What It Unlocks
- **Ship Shields**: Absorb damage per hit (Deflector Class I to XV)
- **Planetary Shields**: Protect colonies from bombardment (Class V to XX)
- **Personal Shields**: Passive ground combat bonuses (+10 to +30)
- **Special Devices**: Repulsor Beam, Cloaking Device, Zyro Shield, Stasis Field, and Black Hole Generator

### Strategy Considerations
- **Defensive Foundation**: Shields keep fleets alive
- **Planetary Protection**: Can't be bombed into submission
- **Turtling**: Extreme defense enables tall empire strategies
- **Cloaking Surprise**: Invisible fleets are devastating

### Race Specializations
- **Hermit Crabs**: Natural defensive bonuses
- **Mice**: Automated repair systems
- **Hamsters**: Balanced shield development

## 6. Planetology

### Purpose
Population growth, terraforming, environmental colonization, and biological warfare.

### What It Unlocks
- **Terraforming**: Improve planet quality (+10 up to +120 population capacity)
- **Soil Enrichment**: Increases planet base size (25% to 50%)
- **Environmental Adaptation**: Ability to colonize hostile worlds (Barren to Radiated)
- **Eco Restoration**: Increases efficiency of pollution cleanup
- **Biological Weapons**: Spores and viruses that kill planetary population

### Strategy Considerations
- **Population = Power**: More people = more production + research + votes
- **Quality over Quantity**: Terraform makes small empires viable
- **Rapid Expansion**: Cloning fills new colonies instantly
- **Domination Victory**: Controlling 2/3 population wins

### Race Specializations
- **Rabbits**: +100% growth stacks with cloning tech
- **Ants**: Rapid industrialization of new colonies
- **Hermit Crabs**: Don't need terraforming (colonize anything)

## Technology Synergies

### Offensive Synergy
**Weapons + Computers + Propulsion** = Devastating Attack Fleet
- High-damage weapons
- Accurate targeting
- Fast enough to catch enemies

### Defensive Synergy
**Force Fields + Construction + Computers** = Impenetrable Defense
- Strong shields
- Heavy armor
- ECM jamming

### Economic Synergy
**Construction + Planetology** = Industrial Juggernaut
- Many factories
- Large populations
- High output

### Expansion Synergy
**Propulsion + Planetology** = Rapid Colonization
- Fast colony ships
- Quick population growth
- Claim galaxy before others

### Espionage Synergy
**Computers + Force Fields** = Information Dominance
- Steal technologies
- See enemy movements
- Cloak your own fleets

## Tech Tree Randomization

Not all techs appear in every game. At each tier, you get **2-3 random options**:

### Example: Weapons Tier 4
You might see:
- Option A: Fusion Beam (strong beam weapon)
- Option B: Scatter Pack Missiles (multi-target)
- Option C: Fusion Bomb (better planetary bombing)

But in another game, you might see:
- Option A: Neutron Blaster (armor-piercing)
- Option B: Pulson Missile (fast missiles)
- Option C: Bio Weapon (disease bomb)

This creates **replayability** - each game has different tech paths available.

## Technology and Victory Conditions

### Domination Victory (Military Conquest)
- **Weapons**: Destroy enemy fleets
- **Construction**: Outproduce everyone
- **Force Fields**: Survive attacks
- **Propulsion**: Project power across galaxy

### Diplomatic Victory (Council Election)
- **Planetology**: Large population = more votes
- **All Fields**: Balanced power impresses other races
- **Computers**: Spy on rivals, know their intentions
