# Propulsion Technology - Complete Tech Tree

## Overview

The Propulsion technology field governs ship movement, including interstellar speed (parsecs/turn), combat maneuverability, fuel range, and special tactical movement systems. It is one of six technology fields.

**Key Races:**
- Budgies: +1 combat speed, +3 initiative
- Rabbits: Rapid expansion (colony ship speed +1)
- Chameleons: Stealth benefits from high maneuverability

**Technology Tiers:** 1-18 (50 RP to 50,000 RP)

---

## Tech Tree Structure

In each game, players are offered **2-3 random technologies** at each tier. Not all technologies appear in every game, creating strategic variety.

### Tier Distribution

| Tier Range | Tech Level | Game Phase | Typical RP Cost |
|------------|------------|------------|-----------------|
| 1-3 | 1-8 | Early | 50-150 RP |
| 4-6 | 9-16 | Early-Mid | 250-800 RP |
| 7-9 | 17-24 | Mid | 1,500-4,000 RP |

| 13-15 | 33-40 | Late | 14,000-24,000 RP |

### Engines

Determine maximum range from friendly colonies.

## Racial Propulsion Bonuses

### Budgies: Superior Pilots
- +1 Combat Speed on all ships
- +3 Initiative in combat
- Ships always act early in turn order

### Rabbits: Rapid Expansion
- Colony ships get +1 speed
- Scout ships get +1 speed
- First to reach new systems

### Chameleons: Evasive Maneuvers
- +1 Maneuver rating on all ships
- Stacks with cloaking for extreme evasion

---

## Strategic Importance

### Early Game (Tiers 1-4)
**Critical Priority:** Fuel range is the #1 constraint
- **Range 3:** Can only reach nearby systems
- **Range 4-5:** Opens mid-range expansion
- **Priority:** Nuclear Engines + Extended Fuel

### Mid Game (Tiers 5-9)
**Focus:** Combat mobility and special systems
- **Ion/Antimatter Drives:** Serious combat advantage
- **Sub-Space Teleporter:** Tactical game-changer
- **Warp Dissipator:** Trap and destroy enemy fleets

### Late Game (Tiers 10-15)
**Focus:** Ultimate mobility
- **Thorium Cells:** Infinite range eliminates logistics
- **Hyper-X Drive:** Speed 8 dominates map
- **Displacement Device:** Ships become very hard to kill
