# Propulsion Technology

## Overview
Propulsion governs the speed of your fleets between stars (Speed), the maximum distance they can travel from a base (Range), and their tactical maneuverability and specialized movement in combat.

**Key Races**: Budgies (+1 movement range), Rabbits (Rapid Expansion)

## Tactical Strategy

**The Range Race**: Early game is dominated by fuel range. If you cannot reach a planet, you cannot claim it. Thorium Cells eliminate this restriction entirely.
**Speed as a Weapon**: Faster ships allow you to strike targets before the enemy can reinforce, or retreat from losing battles.
**Teleporter Supremacy**: The Sub-Space Teleporter is a game-changer, allowing your heavy capital ships to bypass enemy formations and strike vulnerable support ships or bombardment targets immediately.
