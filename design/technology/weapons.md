# Weapons Technology - Complete Tech Tree

## Overview

The Weapons technology field provides offensive capabilities for ships and ground forces. It is one of six technology fields and contains beam weapons, missiles, torpedoes, bombs, biological weapons, and personal combat gear.

**Key Races:**
- Ferrets: +25% weapon damage
- Guinea Pigs: +50% ground combat

**Technology Tiers:** 1-20 (50 RP to 100,000 RP)

---

## Tech Tree Structure

In each game, players are offered **2-3 random technologies** at each tier. Not all technologies appear in every game, creating strategic variety.

### Tier Distribution

| Tier Range | Tech Level | Game Phase | Typical RP Cost |
|------------|------------|------------|-----------------|
| 1-3 | 1-8 | Early | 50-150 RP |
| 4-6 | 9-15 | Early-Mid | 250-800 RP |
| 7-9 | 16-22 | Mid | 1,500-4,000 RP |
| 10-12 | 23-30 | Mid-Late | 6,000-10,000 RP |
| 13-15 | 31-38 | Late | 14,000-24,000 RP |
| 16-18 | 39-45 | End | 30,000-50,000 RP |
| 19-20 | 46-55 | Ultimate | 70,000-100,000 RP |

## Technology Selection

### Available Technologies by Tier

At each tier, players choose from 2-3 randomly selected technologies:

```
Tier 1: Always start with Laser + Nuclear Missile
Tier 2-20: Choose from random selection
```

**Rats Bonus:** Always see 3 choices (Academic Network ability)

### Technology Availability Pool

Each tier has a "pool" of possible technologies. The game randomly selects which are available:

| Tier | Pool Size | Choices Offered |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| 1 | 3 | All (starting tech) |
| 2 | 4 | 2-3 |
| 3 | 2 | 2 |
| 4 | 5 | 2-3 |
| 5 | 4 | 2-3 |
| 6 | 3 | 2-3 |
| 7 | 3 | 2-3 |
| 8 | 3 | 2-3 |
| 9 | 4 | 2-3 |
| 10 | 3 | 2-3 |
| 11 | 2 | 2 |
| 12 | 2 | 2 |
| 13 | 2 | 2 |
| 14 | 4 | 2-3 |
| 15 | 2 | 2 |
| 16 | 2 | 2 |
| 17 | 2 | 2 |
| 18+ | 1 | 1 (guaranteed) |

## Racial Weapon Bonuses

### Ferrets: Deadly Accuracy
- All weapon damage +25%
- Applies to beam, missile, torpedo
- Does NOT apply to bombs (bombardment)

### Guinea Pigs: Ground Supremacy
- Ground combat weapon bonuses +50%
- Hand Lasers: +7.5 (instead of +5)
- Plasma Rifle: +37.5 (instead of +25)

## Weapon Categories Summary

### Beam Weapons (22 total)
Direct fire, instant hit, cannot be intercepted.

| Category | Count | Tech Range | Notes |
|----------|-------|------------|-------|
| Light Beams | 6 | 1-13 | Laser through Neutron Blaster |
| Medium Beams | 6 | 15-27 | Graviton through Phasor |
| Heavy Beams | 5 | 30-42 | Heavy Phasor through Plasma Cannon |
| Ultimate Beams | 5 | 45-55 | Death Ray through Stellar Converter |

### Missiles (11 total)
Tracking projectiles, can be intercepted.

| Category | Count | Tech Range | Notes |
|----------|-------|------------|-------|
| Basic Missiles | 4 | 1-10 | Nuclear through Scatter Pack V |
| Advanced Missiles | 4 | 12-21 | Merculite through Pulson |
| Elite Missiles | 3 | 24-33 | Hercular through Scatter Pack X |

### Torpedoes (4 total)
Heavy warheads, cannot be intercepted, fire every 2 turns.

| Tech Level | Torpedo | Damage |
|------------|---------|--------|
| 25 | Anti-Matter | 30 |
| 35 | Hellfire | 25 (+10 vs shields) |
| 42 | Proton | 40 |
| 48 | Plasma | 75 |

### Bombs (5 total)
Planetary bombardment weapons.

| Tech Level | Bomb | Damage |
|------------|------|--------|
| 3 | Nuclear | 3-12 |
| 10 | Fusion | 5-20 |
| 20 | Anti-Matter | 10-40 |
| 30 | Omega-V | 15-60 |
| 43 | Neutronium | 30-125 |

### Biological Weapons (3 total)
Kill population, permanent damage, diplomatic penalty.

**Note:** Bio weapons are researched in the **Planetology** technology field, not here.

| Tech Level (Planetology) | Weapon | Kill Rate | Max Pop Reduction |

| 9 | Death Spores | 1M per combat round | -10% permanent |
| 25 | Doom Virus | 2M per combat round | -25% permanent |
| 33 | Bio Terminator | 3M per combat round | -50% permanent |

### Ground Combat Weapons (6 total)

| Tech Level | Weapon | Bonus |

| 12 | Gatling Laser Rifle | +10 |
| 18 | Fusion Rifle | +15 |
| 27 | Hand Phasors | +20 |
| 38 | Plasma Rifle | +25 |
| 50 | Mauler Pistol | +30 |

## Strategic Notes

### Early Game (Tiers 1-4)
- **Priority:** Gatling Laser (multi-attack) or Ion Cannon (shield bypass)
- **Avoid:** Ground weapons unless planning invasion
- **Key choice:** Beam vs Missile focus

### Mid Game (Tiers 5-9)
- **Priority:** Fusion Beam, Phasor, Anti-Matter Torpedoes
- **Consider:** Death Spores if enemy has high population
- **Key choice:** Heavy single-shot vs multi-attack

### Late Game (Tiers 10-14)
- **Priority:** Heavy Phasor, Gauss Autocannon, Proton Torpedo
- **Consider:** Neutronium Bomb for conquest
- **Key choice:** Quantity vs overwhelming power

### End Game (Tiers 15-18)
- **Priority:** Death Ray, Mauler Device
- **Ultimate:** Stellar Converter (if available)
- **Key choice:** Win condition weapons
