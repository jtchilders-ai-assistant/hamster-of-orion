# Weapons Technology

## Overview
Weapons technology provides the offensive power of your fleets and ground forces. It is divided into Beam weapons (direct fire), Missiles (tracking, but can be intercepted), Bombs (planetary bombardment), and Personal weaponry for ground combat.

**Key Races**: Ferrets (+25% Weapon Damage), Guinea Pigs (+50% Ground Combat)

## Tactical Strategy

**The Counter-Play**: If the enemy has Class X Shields (-10 dmg), your Nuclear Missiles (4 dmg) and Lasers (1-4 dmg) will do zero damage. You must upgrade to higher-damage weapons like Ion Cannons or Antimatter Missiles.
**Point Defense**: Ships with high initiative and fast-firing weapons (like Gatling Lasers) can effectively shoot down incoming missiles before they land.
**Bombing vs Invading**: Bombing destroys infrastructure (factories). Invading preserves it but is much costlier in lives. Choose your method based on whether you want to capture or merely neutralize the planet.
