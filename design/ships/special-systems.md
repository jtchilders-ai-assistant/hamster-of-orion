# Special Systems

## Cloaking Device
**Effect**: Ship invisible to enemies  
**Limitation**: Decloaks when firing  
**Cost**: 12,000 RP  
**Race Favorite**: Chameleons  

## Perfect Cloaking
**Effect**: Stay invisible even while attacking  
**Cost**: 40,000 RP  
**Strategy**: Ultimate stealth  

## Automated Repair
**Effect**: Regenerate 10-15% HP per turn  
**Cost**: 25,000 RP  
**Race Favorite**: Mice  

## Repulsor Beam
**Effect**: Push enemy ships away  
**Use**: Prevent bombardment  
**Strategy**: Defensive planets  

## Stasis Field
**Effect**: Disable enemy weapons 2 turns  
**Use**: Neutralize biggest threat  
**Strategy**: Tactical control  

## Black Hole Generator
**Effect**: Instant kill one ship  
**Limitation**: Once per battle  
**Cost**: 50,000 RP or discover  

## Transporters
**Effect**: Board enemy ships  
**Mechanic**: Capture instead of destroy  
**Strategy**: Salvage enemy technology
