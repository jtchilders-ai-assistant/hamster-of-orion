# Ship Classes

## Overview
Ships come in different size classes, each with distinct roles and capabilities. Larger ships cost more but carry more weapons and systems. Ship class availability is unlocked through Construction technology.

**Design Philosophy**: MOO1-style simplicity - size matters, but small ships have roles too.

---

## Ship Size Classes

### Scout
**Space**: 50
**Availability**: Always available
**Cost**: ~50-100 BC (very cheap)
**Role**: Exploration, intelligence gathering
**Combat Value**: Minimal - dies to anything
**Best Use**: Early game exploration, late game sensor platforms

**Typical Loadout**:
- Basic engine (speed is life)
- Scanner systems
- Maybe 1 small weapon for defense
- ECM or cloaking (if available)

**Strategy**: Build lots early game. Scouts reveal the map and find colonizable planets. In late game, use as cheap sensor pickets or decoys.

**Race Notes**:
- Budgies: Superior scouts due to +1 movement
- Chameleons: Cloaked scouts are perfect spies
- Everyone needs scouts early

---

### Fighter
**Space**: 100
**Availability**: Always available
**Cost**: ~100-200 BC (cheap)
**Role**: Swarm tactics, point defense, harassment
**Combat Value**: Low individually, strong in numbers
**Best Use**: Swarm enemy capital ships, intercept missiles

**Typical Loadout**:
- Fast engines
- 1-2 light weapons (lasers, light missiles)
- Minimal armor/shields
- High speed/maneuverability

**Strategy**: Build in large numbers. 20 fighters can overwhelm 1 cruiser through sheer volume of fire. Excellent for Rabbits and Ants (quantity races).

**Race Notes**:
- Budgies: Elite fighters with +50% combat = devastating swarms
- Rabbits: Mass fighter production
- Ferrets: Each fighter hits like a cruiser (+25% damage)
- Guinea Pigs: Avoid (prefer heavy ships)

---

### Destroyer
**Space**: 250
**Availability**: Always available
**Cost**: ~300-600 BC (moderate)
**Role**: Mainline combat, versatile workhorse
**Combat Value**: Medium - balanced offense/defense
**Best Use**: Early-mid game backbone of fleets

**Typical Loadout**:
- Balanced weapons (2-3 medium weapons)
- Moderate armor and shields
- Decent engines
- Room for special systems

**Strategy**: Workhorses of early game. Can fight, can defend colonies, can carry bombs for invasion. Most flexible class.

**Race Notes**:
- Standard for most races
- Hamsters: Destroyer specialists (balanced warfare)
- Good transition from fighters to capital ships

---

### Cruiser
**Space**: 500
**Availability**: Ship Construction tech (3,000 RP)
**Cost**: ~800-1,500 BC (expensive)
**Role**: Heavy combat, fleet flagship
**Combat Value**: High - serious firepower
**Best Use**: Mid game power projection

**Typical Loadout**:
- 4-6 heavy weapons
- Strong armor/shields
- Special systems (ECM, repair, etc.)
- Can carry significant bomb payload

**Strategy**: Mid-game power spike. When you get cruisers and enemy doesn't, you win battles easily. First major Construction tech milestone.

**Race Notes**:
- Ferrets: Cruiser-sized ships with battleship firepower
- Guinea Pigs: Heavy armor makes them tanks
- Mice: Automated cruisers (no crew needed)

---

### Battle Cruiser
**Space**: 1,000
**Availability**: Battle Station tech (6,000 RP)
**Cost**: ~2,000-4,000 BC (very expensive)
**Role**: Fleet command, heavy assault
**Combat Value**: Very High - dominates battles
**Best Use**: Late game fleet centerpiece

**Typical Loadout**:
- 8-12 heavy/special weapons
- Maximum armor/shields
- Multiple special systems
- Can glass planets from orbit

**Strategy**: Late-game fleet anchors. One battle cruiser = five destroyers. Expensive but worth it when you can afford them.

**Race Notes**:
- Ants: Can actually afford to mass-produce these
- Rats: Will have better tech, smaller numbers
- Symbol of industrial might

---

### Dreadnought
**Space**: 1,500
**Availability**: Star Fortress tech (18,000 RP)
**Cost**: ~4,000-8,000 BC (extremely expensive)
**Role**: Flagship, planet destroyer, ultimate weapon
**Combat Value**: Extreme - nearly unstoppable
**Best Use**: End game superweapons

**Typical Loadout**:
- 15-20+ weapons (mix of everything)
- Maximum armor + maximum shields
- All special systems
- Stellar Converter or Death Ray
- Self-repair systems

**Strategy**: End-game "I win" buttons. One dreadnought can destroy entire fleets. Losing one is catastrophic. Build only when economy can support it.

**Race Notes**:
- Guinea Pigs: Adamantium dreadnoughts are nightmares
- Hermit Crabs: Mobile fortresses
- Ferrets: Glass cannons of death
- Most races only field 2-5 at a time

---

### Titan (Megaship)
**Space**: 2,500
**Availability**: Megastructure Engineering (40,000 RP)
**Cost**: ~10,000-20,000 BC (bankrupting)
**Role**: Superweapon, mobile starbase
**Combat Value**: Godlike - can solo entire empires
**Best Use**: Victory demonstration, Orion assault

**Typical Loadout**:
- 30+ weapons of all types
- Every defensive system
- Every special system
- Multiple redundant systems
- Can destroy planets, stars, fleets

**Strategy**: Ultimate late-game weapon. Building one is a statement: "I have won." Usually only seen in very long games or right before Orion assault.

**Race Notes**:
- Ants: Might build 2-3 if game goes long enough
- Most races: 0-1 ever
- Symbol of complete dominance

## Fleet Composition Strategies

### Early Game (Turns 1-40)
**Scouts + Fighters + Destroyers**
- 5-10 scouts (exploration)
- 10-20 fighters (defense/harassment)
- 5-10 destroyers (main force)
- Total cost: ~5,000 BC

**Strategy**: Quantity over quality. Map exploration and basic defense.

### Mid Game (Turns 40-80)
**Destroyers + Cruisers**
- 2-5 scouts (ongoing intel)
- 10-15 destroyers (screening force)
- 5-8 cruisers (main punch)
- Total cost: ~15,000 BC

**Strategy**: Quality improving. Cruisers handle serious threats.

### Late Game (Turns 80-120)
**Cruisers + Battle Cruisers**
- 5-10 cruisers (screening/support)
- 3-5 battle cruisers (heavy firepower)
- Maybe 1 dreadnought (if affordable)
- Total cost: ~30,000 BC

**Strategy**: Capital ship focus. Destroyers obsolete.

### End Game (Turns 120+)
**Battle Cruisers + Dreadnoughts**
- 5-10 battle cruisers (standard)
- 2-4 dreadnoughts (fleet anchors)
- Maybe 1 titan (if going for Orion)
- Total cost: ~60,000+ BC

**Strategy**: Only capital ships matter. Everything else dies instantly.

## Race-Specific Fleet Doctrines

### Budgies (Fighter Aces)
**Doctrine**: Small, fast, deadly
- Heavy fighter emphasis
- Cruisers max (rarely bigger)
- Speed and maneuverability over armor
- Hit-and-run tactics

**Typical Fleet**: 30 fighters, 10 cruisers

### Guinea Pigs (Dreadnought Doctrine)
**Doctrine**: Biggest, toughest ships possible
- Skip fighters entirely
- Minimum destroyers
- Rush to dreadnoughts ASAP
- Slow but unstoppable

**Typical Fleet**: 5 dreadnoughts, 10 cruisers

### Chameleons (Asymmetric Warfare)
**Doctrine**: Whatever we stole + cloaking
- Mix of stolen designs
- Heavy cloaking emphasis
- Hit weakest targets
- Avoid fair fights

**Typical Fleet**: Variable, always cloaked

### Hamsters (Balanced Fleet)
**Doctrine**: Mix of everything
- Scouts for intel
- Fighters for screening
- Destroyers and cruisers as backbone
- 1-2 capital ships as flagships

**Typical Fleet**: 5 scouts, 15 fighters, 20 destroyers, 10 cruisers, 2 dreadnoughts

### Ants (Numerical Superiority)
**Doctrine**: Maximum ships, maximum production
- Mass produce everything
- Quantity overwhelms quality
- Replace losses instantly
- Can afford multiple dreadnought fleets

**Typical Fleet**: 100+ ships of all classes

### Mice (Tech Superiority)
**Doctrine**: Fewer but vastly superior ships
- Small fleet
- Best technology
- Automated systems
- Each ship worth 2-3 enemy ships

**Typical Fleet**: 20 ships total, all cutting-edge

### Ferrets (Alpha Strike)
**Doctrine**: Maximum firepower, minimum defense
- Cruiser-heavy
- All weapons, minimal armor
- First strike capability
- Delete enemy before they can respond

**Typical Fleet**: 15 weapon-heavy cruisers

### Rats (Research Fleet)
**Doctrine**: Whatever tech we researched
- Will have best ships eventually
- Small numbers early
- Overwhelming tech advantage late
- Quality over quantity

**Typical Fleet**: 10 ships with tech 2-3 tiers ahead

### Rabbits (Swarm Doctrine)
**Doctrine**: Maximum numbers, minimum cost
- Fighters and destroyers only
- Never build capital ships
- 500 cheap ships > 50 expensive ships
- Overwhelm through numbers

**Typical Fleet**: 200+ fighters, 100+ destroyers

### Hermit Crabs (Fortress Doctrine)
**Doctrine**: Unkillable defensive juggernauts
- Slow, heavily armored
- Maximum shields + maximum armor
- Don't need many ships if they never die
- Patient defensive warfare

**Typical Fleet**: 5 dreadnoughts (each worth 20 normal dreadnoughts)

## Ship Design Slots

Each ship has a certain amount of "space" that must be filled with:

1. **Engine** (required) - 10-20% of ship space
2. **Weapons** (optional) - 30-60% for combat ships
3. **Armor** (automatic) - Covers entire hull
4. **Shields** (optional) - 5-15% of space
5. **Special Systems** (optional) - 10-30% of space
6. **Computers** (optional) - 5-10% of space

**Total Space**: Must equal ship class size exactly

**Example Destroyer (250 space)**:
- Engine: 40 space (Ion Drive)
- Weapons: 120 space (3x Heavy Laser @40 each)
- Shields: 30 space (Class III)
- Battle Computer: 10 space (Mark II)
- ECM: 20 space (Jammer I)
- Remaining: 30 space (armor fills this)

## Ship Naming Conventions

Each race has thematic naming schemes (see species files for details):

**Budgies**: FAS (Free Aeria Ship) + Flight terms
**Guinea Pigs**: GPS + Strength terms
**Chameleons**: CSS + Deception terms
**Hamsters**: HSS + Friendly terms
**Ants**: CAS + Function codes
**Mice**: MCS + Technical terms
**Ferrets**: FHS + Hunter terms
**Rats**: RSS + Scientific terms
**Rabbits**: RCS + Pastoral terms
**Hermit Crabs**: HCS + Mineral terms

**Example**: "FAS Thunderhead" (Budgie Battle Cruiser)

## Build Time Modifiers

Base build time can be modified by:
- **Planetary Production**: Higher production = faster build
- **Automated Factories**: Reduces time
- **Stargates**: Ships built faster
- **Rush Production**: Pay extra BC to speed up

**Formula**: Build Time = (Ship Cost ÷ Planetary Production) × Modifiers
