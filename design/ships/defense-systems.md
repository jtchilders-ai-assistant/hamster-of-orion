# Defense Systems

## Shields
**Function**: Absorb damage before hull  
**HP**: 25 (Class I) → 250 (Planetary)  
**Regeneration**: 100% between battles  
**Strategy**: Essential for survival  

---

## Armor
**Function**: Hull hit points  
**HP**: 5/space (Titanium) → 60/space (Adamantium Exo)  
**Regeneration**: None (permanent damage)  
**Strategy**: Last line of defense  

---

## ECM (Electronic Countermeasures)
**Function**: Reduces enemy accuracy  
**Effect**: -10% to -35% enemy hit chance  
**Strategy**: Stacks with maneuverability  

---

## Point Defense
**Function**: Shoot down incoming missiles  
**Success Rate**: 30-70% per missile  
**Strategy**: Counter missile-heavy enemies
