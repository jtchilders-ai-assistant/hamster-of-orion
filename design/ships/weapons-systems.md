# Weapon Systems Behavior

## Beam Weapons
**Mechanics**: Instant hit if accuracy succeeds  
**Advantage**: Can't be intercepted  
**Disadvantage**: Damage decreases with range  
**Best For**: Reliable damage output  

**Examples**: Laser, Plasma, Phasor, Death Ray

---

## Missile Weapons
**Mechanics**: Projectile travels to target  
**Advantage**: Consistent damage at all ranges  
**Disadvantage**: Can be shot down  
**Best For**: Long-range bombardment  

**Examples**: Nuclear, Fusion, Antimatter, Pulson

---

## Special Weapons
**Mechanics**: Unique effects  
**Examples**:
- Mauler Device: Ignores shields
- Stellar Converter: Destroys planets
- Bio Weapon: Kills population only

---

## Weapon Targeting AI
Ships auto-target nearest threat unless player directs otherwise.  
Priority: Bombers > Huge ships > Medium ships > Small ships
