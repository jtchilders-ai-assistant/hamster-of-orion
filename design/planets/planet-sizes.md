# Planet Sizes

## Overview
Planets in MOO1 come in five size categories. Size determines the base maximum population capacity for a colony. 

## Size Categories

| Size | Planet capacity statistical distribution (Base Capacity) | Frequency | Description |
|------|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Tiny** | 10 - 20 | 15% | Moon-sized worlds. |
| **Small** | 25 - 40 | 25% | Small planets. |
| **Medium** | 45 - 70 | 30% | Earth-sized worlds. |
| **Large** | 75 - 100| 20% | Large terrestrial worlds. |
| **Huge** | 100 - 120| 10% | Super-terrestrial worlds. |

---

## Combined Effects
Total population capacity is calculated based on **Planet Size + Environment Type**.
- **Terran Huge**: Highest capacity world.
- **Radiated Tiny**: Lowest capacity world.

## Terraforming
Planetology technology can increase a planet's maximum population capacity through terraforming (e.g., +10, +20, etc. per tech level).
