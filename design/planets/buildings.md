# Planetary Buildings

## Overview
Buildings provide bonuses to production, research, defense, and growth. Limited by planet size and resources.

---

## Core Buildings (Available to All)

### Factory
**Cost**: 10 BC per factory
**Effect**: Converts population to production
**Limit**: Based on planet size and waste tech
**Required**: For all production

**Race Modifiers**:
- Ants: Produce 2.5× output at high tech
- Mice: Automated, fewer needed

### Research Lab
**Cost**: 50 BC
**Effect**: +2 research per scientist  
**Limit**: One per 2 population
**Strategy**: Essential for tech advancement

### Hydroponic Farm
**Cost**: 30 BC
**Effect**: +2 food per farmer
**Tech Required**: Biotechnology
**Strategy**: Support larger populations

---

## Advanced Buildings

### Cloning Center
**Cost**: 200 BC
**Effect**: +50% population growth
**Tech Required**: 3,500 RP
**Strategy**: Rapid colony development
**Race Bonus**: Rabbits get +150% total

### Planetary Shield
**Cost**: 500 BC
**Effect**: Absorbs 50-200 bombardment damage/turn
**Tech Required**: 5,000-35,000 RP
**Strategy**: Fortress worlds

### Missile Base
**Cost**: 150 BC per base
**Effect**: Defends against invasion/bombardment
**Limit**: 20-50 depending on tech
**Strategy**: Heavily defend border worlds

### Star Gate  
**Cost**: 2,000 BC
**Effect**: Ships built here +2 parsecs/turn permanently
**Tech Required**: 25,000 RP
**Strategy**: Build on high-production worlds
**Limitation**: One per planet

---

## Special Buildings

### Grand University (Rats Unique)
**Cost**: 500 BC
**Effect**: +100% research output
**Strategy**: Rats' research becomes absurd

### Galactic Embassy (Hamsters Unique)
**Cost**: 300 BC
**Effect**: +20% diplomatic relations
**Strategy**: Peaceful expansion

### Hive Complex (Ants Unique)  
**Cost**: 400 BC
**Effect**: +50% production, +25 max pop
**Strategy**: Production powerhouse

### War Academy (Guinea Pigs Unique)
**Cost**: 300 BC
**Effect**: Ground troops 2× effectiveness
**Strategy**: Conquest support

---

## Building Strategy

**Early Game**: Factories > Research Labs  
**Mid Game**: Defenses, Cloning Centers  
**Late Game**: Star Gates, Special Buildings
