# Planet Types

## Overview
Planets in Master of Orion (MOO1) are defined by their **Environment Type** and **Mineral Richness**. These factors determine habitability, population capacity, and industrial productivity.

---

## Environment Types (Habitability)

There are multiple environment types, from most hostile to most hospitable. Hostile environments require specific Planetology technology to colonize.

### Hostile Environments (Require Tech)
Requires Controlled Environment technology from the Planetology tree to colonize.

| Environment | Tech Required | Notes |
|-------------|---------------|-------|
| Radiated | Controlled Radiated | Most hostile |
| Toxic | Controlled Toxic | Corrosive atmosphere |
| Inferno | Controlled Inferno | Extreme heat |
| Dead | Controlled Dead | No atmosphere |
| Tundra | Controlled Tundra | Frozen |
| Barren | Controlled Barren | Lifeless rock |

### Standard Environments (No Tech Required)
Can be colonized from game start.

| Environment | Habitability | Notes |
|-------------|--------------|-------|
| Minimal | Low | Barely supports life |
| Desert | Low-Medium | Scarce water |
| Steppe | Medium | Rugged terrain |
| Arid | Medium | Limited water |
| Ocean | Good | Mostly water |
| Jungle | Good | Lush vegetation |
| Terran | Excellent | Earth-like, ideal |

### Legendary Environment
| Environment | Habitability | Notes |
|-------------|--------------|-------|
| Gaia | Perfect | Ideal conditions, rare |

## Mineral Richness (Production)

Independent of environment, each planet has a mineral richness level that affects factory output:

| Richness | Production Modifier | Notes |
|----------|---------------------|-------|
| Ultra Poor | ×1/3 | Very slow development |
| Poor | ×1/2 | Limited resources |
| Normal | ×1 | Standard (~most planets) |
| Rich | ×2 | Construction speed doubled |
| Ultra Rich | ×3 | Exceptional production |

**Strategic Value**: Rich and Ultra Rich planets are extremely valuable for ship production.

---

## Planet Size

Determines base maximum population:

| Size | Base Max Pop Range |
|------|-------------------|
| Tiny | 10-20 |
| Small | 30-40 |
| Medium | 50-70 |
| Large | 80-100 |
| Huge | 110-150+ |

---

## Colonization Requirements

**Standard Environments**: Can colonize with any colony ship from game start.

**Hostile Environments**: Require researching the appropriate "Controlled [Environment]" technology in the Planetology field before colonization is possible.

**Terraforming**: Planetology technology can also improve planets:
- Increase maximum population capacity
- Some advanced techs can improve environment type

---

## Special Planet Types

### Artifacts World
- Contains remnants of ancient civilization
- **One-time bonus**: Colonizing gives a free technology breakthrough
- **Ongoing bonus**: 2x research output from this planet
- High priority colonization target

### Orion
- Located near galactic center
- Guarded by the powerful Guardian ship
- **Must defeat Guardian first** before colonizing
- **Rewards after defeating Guardian**:
  - Access to excellent planet
  - Multiple advanced technologies
  - **Ongoing bonus**: 4x research output from Orion

---

## Environment and Growth

Population growth rate is affected by environment:
- Hostile environments: Slower growth
- Terran/Gaia: Faster growth
- Exact rates depend on tech level and racial bonuses
