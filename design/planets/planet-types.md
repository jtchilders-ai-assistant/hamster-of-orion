# Planet Types

## Overview
Planets in Master of Orion (MOO1) are defined by their **Environment Type** and **Resource Level**. These factors determine habitability, population growth, and industrial productivity.

---

## Environment Types (Habitability)

There are 14 environment types, listed from most hostile to most hospitable. Hostile environments require specific technology to colonize and have halved population growth rates.

### Hostile Environments (Require Tech)
Requires **Deep Space / Environment Suits** technology to land. Population growth is **0.5x**.

1. **Radiated**: The most hostile. Requires highest tech level to inhabit.
2. **Toxic**: Highly corrosive atmosphere.
3. **Inferno**: Extremely high temperatures.
4. **Dead**: No atmosphere or water.
5. **Tundra**: Frozen world.
6. **Barren**: Lifeless rock.

### Standard Environments (No Tech Required)
Accessible from the start of the game. Population growth is **1.0x**.

7. **Minimal**: Oxygen poor, barely supports life.
8. **Desert**: Scarce water, dust storms.
9. **Steppe**: Rugged terrain, difficult to clear.
10. **Arid**: Limited water, dry landscapes. 
11. **Ocean**: Mostly water, few land masses.
12. **Jungle**: Lush vegetation, young worlds.
13. **Terran**: Earth-like, ideal for life.

### Legendary Environment
14. **Gaia**: Perfection. Population growth is **2.0x**.

## Resource Levels (Production)

Independent of environment, a planet has one of five resource levels determining industrial output for ship, defense, and factory construction.

| Level | Modifier | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Ultra Poor** | 1/3x | Extremely scarce minerals. Very slow development. |
| **Poor** | 1/2x | Limited resources. |
| **Normal** | 1x | Standard mineral deposits (~90% of planets). |
| **Rich** | 2x | Abundant heavy metals. Construction speed doubled. |
| **Ultra Rich** | 3x | Mineral wealth beyond measure. Construction speed tripled. |

---

## Colonization & Technology

- **Starting Range**: Players can reach planets within 3 parsecs. Propulsion research extends this range.
- **Environment Tech**: Hostile worlds require "Controlled [Environment] Environment" tech (e.g., Controlled Barren Environment).
- **Terraforming**: Advanced Biotechnology can improve a planet's environment category or base size.

---

## Special Effects

### Artifacts World
- **Research Bonus**: 2x Research Points (RP) generated on this planet.
- **Discovery**: The first race to scout the world gains a random high-level technology immediately.

### Orion
- **Research Bonus**: 4x Research Points (RP).
- **Discovery**: First scout gains multiple high-level technologies.
- **Guardian**: Guarded by a powerful ancient ship.
