# Victory Conditions

## Overview

Hamster of Orion (following MOO1) has **two victory conditions**:

1. **Council Election Victory** - Win a 2/3 majority vote in the High Council
2. **Military Conquest** - Eliminate all other races

There is NO research/transcendence victory in MOO1.

---

## 1. Council Election Victory

### Formation of the High Council

The High Council forms when **50% or more** of habitable planets in the galaxy are colonized (by any combination of races).

Once formed, the Council meets every **25 turns** to hold an election.

### Election Process

1. **Two Candidates**: The two races with the highest total population are nominated
2. **Voting**: All races vote based on their population share
3. **Victory Threshold**: A candidate needs **2/3 (66.67%)** of the total vote to win

### Vote Allocation

Each race's vote weight equals their share of galactic population:
```
Vote_Weight = Race_Population / Total_Galactic_Population
```

### Voting Behavior

Races vote based on:
- **Diplomatic relations** with candidates
- **Alliances** (allies vote for each other)
- **Wars** (enemies vote against)
- **Racial personality** (some races more likely to abstain)

### Abstention

- Races can **abstain** if they dislike both candidates
- Abstained votes still count toward the 2/3 threshold requirement
- If no candidate reaches 2/3, election fails and continues next Council meeting

### Defying the Council

If you **lose** the Council vote but refuse to accept:
- All other races immediately declare war on you
- You must achieve Military Conquest to win
- Very difficult but possible

---

## 2. Military Conquest Victory

### Condition

Eliminate all other races from the galaxy:
- Destroy or capture all their colonies
- Destroy all their ships

### How Races Are Eliminated

A race is eliminated when they have:
- No remaining colonies
- No remaining colony ships

### Strategies

- **Early Rush**: Attack before opponents build up
- **Technological Superiority**: Out-research then overwhelm
- **Economic Dominance**: Out-produce opponents
- **Diplomatic Isolation**: Ally with some, destroy others one by one

---

## The Orion System

### Guardian of Orion

The Orion system is guarded by a powerful automated ship called the **Guardian**. 

**Defeating the Guardian**:
- Requires a strong fleet
- Guardian has advanced weapons and defenses
- Very difficult early/mid game

**Rewards**:
- Access to Orion colony (excellent planet)
- Advanced technologies from the ancient Masters
- Significant advantage but NOT an instant win

**Note**: Defeating the Guardian is NOT a victory condition - it's a strategic milestone that provides major advantages.

---

## Game End Summary

| Victory Type | Condition | Notes |
|--------------|-----------|-------|
| **Council Election** | Win 2/3 vote | Can refuse and fight on |
| **Military Conquest** | Eliminate all races | Can happen anytime |

---

## No Other Victory Types

Unlike later 4X games, MOO1 does NOT have:
- ❌ Research/Technology victory
- ❌ Economic victory
- ❌ Wonder/Achievement victory
- ❌ Score victory
- ❌ Time limit victory

The game continues until Council Election or Military Conquest.
