# Victory Conditions

## Overview
Five paths to victory. Choose your strategy and pursue it relentlessly. Or balance all and discover the hidden sixth path.

---

## 1. Domination Victory

**Requirement**: Control 2/3 (66.7%) of total galactic population

**How to Achieve**:
- Expand rapidly (claim many planets)
- Maximize population growth (Biotechnology)
- Conquer enemy colonies
- Terraform to increase capacity

**Best Races**: Rabbits (+100% growth), Ants (+25% growth + production)

**Timeline**: Turn 120-180 typical

**Strategy**:
- Focus: Biotechnology (cloning, terraforming)
- Build: Colony ships constantly
- Claim: Every system possible
- Grow: Fill planets to maximum

**Rabbits Path**:
- Advanced Cloning (+200% total growth)
- Terraform +60 all planets
- Claim 40-60% of systems
- Fill with rabbit population
- Win Turn 120-150

**Victory Moment**:
> "Your empire spans the galaxy. Two-thirds of all living beings owe allegiance to you. The High Council has no choice but to proclaim you Master of Orion by unanimous consent."

---

## 2. Discovery Victory (Orion)

**Requirement**: Defeat the Guardian, claim the Cosmic Wheel

**How to Achieve**:
- Research advanced weapons
- Research advanced shields
- Research long-range propulsion
- Build powerful fleet (5-10 Dreadnoughts minimum)
- Reach Orion (galactic center, 40-60 parsecs from edge)
- Defeat Guardian
- Land on planet, claim Wheel

**Best Races**: Rats (fast research), Ferrets (maximum damage), Budgies (superior combat)

**Timeline**: Turn 100-150 typical

**The Guardian**:
- Dreadnought-class automated defense
- Death Ray, Stellar Converter, Black Hole Generator
- Adamantium Armor, Planetary Shields
- First Strike capability
- Regenerates 25% HP per turn
- Nearly unbeatable alone

**Minimum Requirements**:
- Propulsion: Ion Drive or Sub-Space Teleporter
- Weapons: Plasma Cannon or better
- Shields: Class V or better
- Fleet: 5+ Dreadnoughts or 10+ Battle Cruisers
- Armor: Neutronium minimum

**Strategy**:
- Rush: Research military tech exclusively
- Build: Concentrated fleet at secret base
- Scout: Locate Orion early
- Strike: One massive assault
- Win or Die: No second chances

**Victory Moment**:
> "The Guardian falls. The barrier dissolves. You descend to Orion's surface and place your paw upon the Cosmic Wheel. Its power flows through you, through your people, through your civilization. The Wheel has chosen. You are the Master of Orion."

---

## 3. Diplomatic Victory (High Council)

**Requirement**: Win 2/3 majority vote in High Council

**How to Achieve**:
- Build alliances (formal treaties)
- Control significant population (votes = population %)
- Maintain honorable reputation
- Bribe/intimidate swing voters
- Wait for Council vote (every 25 turns after 50% colonized)

**Best Races**: Hamsters (+30% diplomacy), Rabbits (population for votes)

**Timeline**: Turn 150-200 typical

**Vote Calculation**:
- Your population: [X%] of galaxy
- Allied votes: [Y%] of galaxy
- Need: 66.7%+ total

**Strategies**:
1. **Alliance Network** (Hamsters):
   - Trade agreements with all
   - Research pacts
   - Defensive pacts
   - Formal alliances with 3-4 races
   - Their votes + yours = 67%+

2. **Population Control** (Rabbits):
   - Control 67%+ personally
   - Vote for yourself
   - Don't need allies
   - Domination-Diplomatic hybrid

3. **Intimidation** (Guinea Pigs):
   - Massive military
   - Threaten all
   - "Vote for me or else"
   - Fear-based support

4. **Bribery** (Rich empires):
   - Buy votes with BC/tech
   - Calculate costs
   - Last-minute deals

**Rejection Option**: Can reject victory and continue fighting

**Victory Moment**:
> "The High Council convenes. One by one, the races cast their votes. The tally rises. Two-thirds achieved. The Council proclaims you Master of Orion. The galaxy bows to your legitimacy."

---

## 4. Survival Victory (Extermination)

**Requirement**: Eliminate all rival civilizations

**How to Achieve**:
- Conquer every enemy colony
- Destroy every enemy ship
- Last empire standing
- Win by default

**Best Races**: Guinea Pigs (warfare), Ferrets (alpha strikes)

**Timeline**: Turn 150-250+ (longest victory)

**Strategy**:
- Total War: Against everyone
- Systematic: Conquer empire by empire
- Ruthless: No mercy, no treaties
- Persistent: Chase down every colony

**Challenges**:
- Everyone unites against you
- Long, grinding warfare
- Easy to get stalemated
- Resource exhaustion

**When to Pursue**:
- Overwhelmingly strong military
- Enemies too divided to unite
- Other victories impossible
- Roleplaying genocidal empire

**Victory Moment**:
> "Silence. The galaxy is empty save for your people. No rival civilizations remain. No opposition. No resistance. You are Master of Orion by simple fact: there is no one left to contest the title."

---

## 5. Transcendence Victory (Hidden)

**Requirement**: Achieve perfect balance - excellence in ALL aspects simultaneously

**How to Achieve**: ???

The game never explicitly states this victory condition. Players must discover it through balanced play.

**Suspected Requirements**:
- High technology (all fields researched to Tier 7+)
- Strong military (fleet power in top 3)
- Large population (top 50% of galaxy)
- Good diplomatic relations (+50 average with living races)
- Economic strength (top 3 in production)
- No atrocities (no bio-weapons, no planet destruction)
- Wisdom threshold (time + decisions + balance)

**Theory**: Orion doesn't need to be conquered - it recognizes worthiness

**Best Races**: Hamsters (balanced), Rats (research everything)

**Timeline**: Turn 180-250+ (requires long game)

**Discovery**:
- No announcement
- Sudden event
- Orion barrier opens
- No Guardian fight
- Invited inside
- Cosmic Wheel activates
- Transcendence begins

**Victory Moment**:
> "You did not claim Orion. Orion claimed you. The barrier falls away. The Wheel calls. You do not descend in conquest but in invitation. The Ancient Ones whisper: 'You are ready. Join us.' The Wheel turns. Your species begins to glow with inner light. One by one, your people step through portals appearing everywhere. You are not destroying. You are becoming. This is not victory. This is evolution."

## Multiple Victory Pursuit

**Can Pursue Multiple**:
- Domination + Diplomatic = Same population goal
- Discovery + Survival = Conquer while preparing Orion
- All paths = Transcendence condition

**Strategic Flexibility**:
- Start one path
- Pivot if circumstances change
- Hedge bets with multiple approaches

**Displays**:
- Victory type achieved

- Hall of Fame entry
- Unique ending cinematic per victory type
- Epilogue text based on race and path

**Post-Victory**:
- Continue playing (sandbox mode)
- Replay with different race
