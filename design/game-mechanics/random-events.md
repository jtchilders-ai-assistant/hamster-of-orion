# Random Events

## Overview
10% chance per turn for random event. Adds variety and challenges to each playthrough.

---

## Space Monsters

### Space Amoeba
**Threat**: Medium
**Behavior**: Regenerates HP, slow movement
**Reward**: Regeneration technology
**Strategy**: Focus fire, kill quickly

### Crystal Entity
**Threat**: High
**Behavior**: Reflects beam weapons back at attacker
**Reward**: Crystal shielding tech
**Strategy**: Use missiles only

### Space Dragon
**Threat**: Very High
**Behavior**: Fire breath, guards treasure hoard
**Reward**: Multiple techs or artifact
**Strategy**: Heavy fleet required

---

## Ancient Discoveries

### Derelict Ship
**Event**: Find abandoned ship in deep space
**Options**: Salvage or Board
**Salvage**: +BC
**Board**: Possible tech, possible trap

### Ancient Ruins
**Event**: Discover Ancient One facility
**Result**: Technology breakthrough (25% chance)
**Benefit**: +50% research on planet

### Time Capsule
**Event**: Ancient message beacon
**Result**: Historical knowledge, inspiration
**Benefit**: +1 free tech choice

---

## Disasters

### Solar Flare
**Event**: Star emits dangerous radiation
**Effect**: Ships in system take 10-30 damage
**Mitigation**: Advanced shields protect

### Plague
**Event**: Disease outbreak on planet
**Effect**: -20% population
**Mitigation**: Bio-tech reduces severity

### Meteor Strike
**Event**: Asteroid impacts colony
**Effect**: -10 factories destroyed
**Mitigation**: Planetary shields prevent

---

## Diplomatic Incidents

### Border Skirmish
**Event**: Ships exchange fire at border
**Options**: Apologize or Escalate
**Apologize**: -10 relations
**Escalate**: Possible war

### Trade Dispute
**Event**: Tariff disagreement
**Options**: Negotiate or Sanctions
**Negotiate**: Fair resolution
**Sanctions**: -20 relations, +10% trade income

---

## Discoveries

### Wormhole Discovery
**Event**: Stable wormhole found
**Effect**: Instant travel to paired system
**Benefit**: Strategic mobility

### Rich Asteroid Belt
**Event**: Mineral-rich asteroids
**Effect**: +25% production in system
**Benefit**: Permanent improvement

---

Random events keep games fresh and unpredictable!
