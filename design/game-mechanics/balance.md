# Design Balance Principles

## Overview
Core design philosophy ensuring fun, strategic depth, and replayability.

---

## Race Balance Philosophy

**No Perfect Race**: Each has strengths AND weaknesses
- Rabbits: Best growth, worst military
- Rats: Best research, weakest economy
- Guinea Pigs: Best ground combat, worst research

**Multiple Paths**: Every race can win every victory
- But some are natural fits
- Ferrets = Discovery (combat Orion)
- Rabbits = Domination (population)

**Counter-Play**: Every strength has a counter
- Chameleon spies vs Ant immunity
- Ferret damage vs Hermit Crab armor
- Rabbit numbers vs Ferret quality

---

## Victory Balance

**No Single Path Dominant**:
- Diplomatic: Requires allies or large population for Council votes
- Domination: Requires military strength to eliminate all rivals

**Timeline Variance**: Different speeds
- Diplomatic: Medium (Turn 100-200, depends on colonization rate)
- Domination: Variable (Turn 80-300+, depends on aggression)

---

## Technology Balance

**Six Fields Equal Weight**:
- Weapons: Offensive power
- Propulsion: Mobility power
- Construction: Economic power
- Computers: Force multiplier
- Force Fields: Defensive power
- Biotechnology: Population power

**Miniaturization**: Old tech stays relevant
**Random Selection**: Can't get everything

---

## Economic Balance

**Production Scaling**: Compounds over time
- Early: Small differences
- Mid: Moderate gaps
- Late: Exponential divergence

**Ant Production**: Most powerful late-game
- But slow early (research penalty)
- Vulnerable to early rush

---

## Military Balance

**Quality vs Quantity**:
- 10 scouts ≠ 1 destroyer
- But 30 fighters can beat 1 cruiser
- Dreadnought can solo 5 cruisers

**Tech Advantage Scaling**:
- 1 tier ahead: +30% effective power
- 2 tiers ahead: +70% effective power
- 3+ tiers ahead: Nearly unstoppable

---

## Player Skill Ceiling

**Easy to Learn**: Basic mechanics simple
**Hard to Master**: Optimal play complex
**Skill Expression**: Multiple decision points per turn
**Strategic Depth**: Long-term planning rewarded
